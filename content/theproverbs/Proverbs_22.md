## Verses 1–16: Proverbs of Solomon

**1**
נִבְחָ֣ר שֵׁ֭ם מֵעֹ֣שֶׁר רָ֑ב מִכֶּ֥סֶף וּ֝מִזָּהָ֗ב חֵ֣ן טֽוֹב׃
*Nivchar shem me-osher rav, mi-kesef u-mi-zahav chen tov.*
Repute is preferable to great wealth,
grace is better than silver and gold.

**2**
עָשִׁ֣יר וָרָ֣שׁ נִפְגָּ֑שׁוּ עֹשֵׂ֖ה כֻלָּ֣ם יְהֹוָֽה׃
*Ashir va-rash nifgashu, oseh kullam Adonai.*
The rich and the poor meet;
G made them both.

**3**
עָר֤וּם רָאָ֣ה רָעָ֣ה וְנִסְתָּ֑ר וּ֝פְתָיִ֗ים עָבְר֥וּ וְֽנֶעֱנָֽשׁוּ׃
*Arum ra'ah ra'ah ve-nistar, u-fetayim avru ve-ne'enshu.*
One who was shrewd saw trouble and took cover;
the simple kept going and paid the penalty.

**4**
עֵ֣קֶב עֲ֭נָוָה יִרְאַ֣ת יְהֹוָ֑ה עֹ֖שֶׁר וְכָב֣וֹד וְחַיִּֽים׃
*Eqev anavah yir'at Adonai, osher ve-khavod ve-chayyim.*
The effect of humility is fear of G,
wealth, honor, and life.

**5**
צִנִּ֣ים פַּ֭חִים בְּדֶ֣רֶךְ עִקֵּ֑שׁ שׁוֹמֵ֥ר נַ֝פְשׁ֗וֹ יִרְחַ֥ק מֵהֶֽם׃
*Tzinnim pachim be-derekh iqqesh, shomer nafsho yirchaq mehem.*
Thorns and snares are in the path of the crooked;
those who value their life will keep far from them.

**6**
חֲנֹ֣ךְ לַ֭נַּעַר עַל־פִּ֣י דַרְכּ֑וֹ גַּ֥ם כִּי־יַ֝זְקִ֗ין לֹא־יָס֥וּר מִמֶּֽנָּה׃
*Chanokh la-na'ar al-pi darko, gam ki-yazqin lo-yasur mimmenah.*
Train children in the way they each ought to go;
they will not swerve from it even in old age.

**7**
עָ֭שִׁיר בְּרָשִׁ֣ים יִמְשׁ֑וֹל וְעֶ֥בֶד לֹ֝וֶ֗ה לְאִ֣ישׁ מַלְוֶֽה׃
*Ashir be-rashim yimsbol, ve-eved loveh le-ish malveh.*
A rich person rules the poor,
and the borrower is a slave to the man who lends.

**8**
זוֹרֵ֣עַ עַ֭וְלָה יִקְצׇר־אָ֑וֶן וְשֵׁ֖בֶט עֶבְרָת֣וֹ יִכְלֶֽה׃
*Zore'a avlah yiqtzor-aven, ve-shevet evrato yikhleh.*
One who sows injustice shall reap misfortune;
the rod of wrath shall fail.

**9**
טֽוֹב־עַ֭יִן ה֣וּא יְבֹרָ֑ךְ כִּֽי־נָתַ֖ן מִלַּחְמ֣וֹ לַדָּֽל׃
*Tov-ayin hu yevorakh, ki-natan mi-lachmo la-dal.*
One who is generous is blessed,
while giving bread to the poor.

**10**
גָּ֣רֵֽשׁ לֵ֭ץ וְיֵצֵ֣א מָד֑וֹן וְ֝יִשְׁבֹּ֗ת דִּ֣ין וְקָלֽוֹן׃
*Garesh letz ve-yetzei madon, ve-yishbot din ve-qalon.*
Expel the scoffer and contention departs,
quarrel and contumely cease.

**11**
אֹהֵ֥ב טְהׇר־לֵ֑ב חֵ֥ן שְׂ֝פָתָ֗יו רֵעֵ֥הוּ מֶֽלֶךְ׃
*Ohev tehar-lev, chen sefatav re'ehu melekh.*
A pure-hearted comrade —
his speech is gracious;
he has the king for his companion.

**12**
עֵינֵ֣י יְ֭הֹוָה נָ֣צְרוּ דָ֑עַת וַ֝יְסַלֵּ֗ף דִּבְרֵ֥י בֹגֵֽד׃
*Einei Adonai natzru da'at, va-yesallef divrei voged.*
The eyes of G watch the person who is wise;
God subverts the words of the treacherous.

**13**
אָמַ֣ר עָ֭צֵל אֲרִ֣י בַח֑וּץ בְּת֥וֹךְ רְ֝חֹב֗וֹת אֵרָצֵֽחַ׃
*Amar atzel ari va-chutz, be-tokh rechovot eratzeach.*
The sluggard says, "There's a lion in the street;
I shall be killed if I step outside."

**14**
שׁוּחָ֣ה עֲ֭מֻקָּה פִּ֣י זָר֑וֹת זְע֥וּם יְ֝הֹוָ֗ה יִפׇּל־שָֽׁם׃
*Shuchah amuqqah pi zarot, ze'um Adonai yippal-sham.*
The mouth of a forbidden woman is a deep pit;
he who is doomed by G falls into it.

**15**
אִ֭וֶּלֶת קְשׁוּרָ֣ה בְלֶב־נָ֑עַר שֵׁ֥בֶט מ֝וּסָ֗ר יַרְחִיקֶ֥נָּה מִמֶּֽנּוּ׃
*Ivvelet qeshurah ve-lev-na'ar, shevet musar yarchiqennah mimmennu.*
If folly settles in the heart of a youth,
the rod of discipline will remove it.

**16**
עֹ֣שֵֽׁק דָּ֭ל לְהַרְבּ֣וֹת ל֑וֹ נֹתֵ֥ן לְ֝עָשִׁ֗יר אַךְ־לְמַחְסֽוֹר׃
*Osheq dal le-harbbot lo, noten le-ashir akh-le-machsor.*
To profit by withholding what is due to the poor
is like making gifts to the rich — pure loss.

## Verses 17–29: The Words of the Sages

**17**
הַ֥ט אׇזְנְךָ֗ וּ֭שְׁמַע דִּבְרֵ֣י חֲכָמִ֑ים וְ֝לִבְּךָ֗ תָּשִׁ֥ית לְדַעְתִּֽי׃
*Hat oznekha u-shema divrei chakhamim, ve-libbkha tashit le-da'ti.*
Incline your ear and listen to the words of the sages;
pay attention to my wisdom.

**18**
כִּֽי־נָ֭עִים כִּֽי־תִשְׁמְרֵ֣ם בְּבִטְנֶ֑ךָ יִכֹּ֥נוּ יַ֝חְדָּ֗ו עַל־שְׂפָתֶֽיךָ׃
*Ki-na'im ki-tishmerem be-vitnekha, yikonu yachdav al-sefatekha.*
It is good that you store them inside you,
and that all of them be constantly on your lips,

**19**
לִֽהְי֣וֹת בַּ֭יְהֹוָה מִבְטַחֶ֑ךָ הוֹדַעְתִּ֖יךָ הַיּ֣וֹם אַף־אָֽתָּה׃
*Li-hyot ba-Adonai mivtachekha, hoda'tikha ha-yom af-attah.*
that you may put your trust in G.
I let you know today — yes, you —

**20**
הֲלֹ֤א כָתַ֣בְתִּֽי לְ֭ךָ שָׁלִישִׁ֑ים בְּמֹ֖עֵצ֣וֹת וָדָֽעַת׃
*Halo khatavti lekha shalishim, be-mo'etzot va-da'at.*
indeed, I wrote down for you a threefold lore,
wise counsel,

**21**
לְהֽוֹדִיעֲךָ֗ קֹ֭שְׁטְ אִמְרֵ֣י אֱמֶ֑ת לְהָשִׁ֥יב אֲמָרִ֥ים אֱ֝מֶ֗ת לְשֹׁלְחֶֽיךָ׃
*Le-hodi'akha qosht imrei emet, le-hashiv amarim emet le-sholechekha.*
to let you know truly reliable words,
that you may give a faithful reply to those who sent you.

**22**
אַֽל־תִּגְזׇל־דָּ֭ל כִּ֣י דַל־ה֑וּא וְאַל־תְּדַכֵּ֖א עָנִ֣י בַשָּֽׁעַר׃
*Al-tigzol-dal ki dal-hu, ve-al-tedhakke ani va-sha'ar.*
Do not rob the wretched because they are wretched;
do not crush the poor in the gate;

**23**
כִּֽי־יְ֭הֹוָה יָרִ֣יב רִיבָ֑ם וְקָבַ֖ע אֶת־קֹבְעֵיהֶ֣ם נָֽפֶשׁ׃
*Ki-Adonai yariv rivam, ve-qava et-qov'eihem nafesh.*
for G will take up their cause
and despoil those who despoil them of life.

**24**
אַל־תִּ֭תְרַע אֶת־בַּ֣עַל אָ֑ף וְאֶת־אִ֥ישׁ חֵ֝מ֗וֹת לֹ֣א תָבֽוֹא׃
*Al-tit'ra et-ba'al af, ve-et-ish chemot lo tavo.*
Do not associate with a hothead,
or go about with a hot-tempered man,

**25**
פֶּן־תֶּאֱלַ֥ף אֹרְחֹתָ֑ו וְלָקַחְתָּ֖ מוֹקֵ֣שׁ לְנַפְשֶֽׁךָ׃
*Pen-te'elaf orchotav, ve-laqachta moqesh le-nafshekha.*
lest you learn their ways
and find yourself ensnared.

**26**
אַל־תְּהִ֥י בְתֹֽקְעֵי־כָ֑ף בַּ֝עֹרְבִ֗ים מַשָּׁאֽוֹת׃
*Al-tehi ve-toq'ei-khaf, ba-orvim mashaot.*
Do not be one of those who give their hand,
who stand surety for debts,

**27**
אִם־אֵֽין־לְךָ֥ לְשַׁלֵּ֑ם לָ֥מָּה יִקַּ֥ח מִ֝שְׁכָּבְךָ֗ מִתַּחְתֶּֽיךָ׃
*Im-ein-lekha le-shallem, lammah yiqqach mishkavkha mi-tachtekha.*
lest your bed be taken from under you
when you have no money to pay.

**28**
אַל־תַּ֭סֵּג גְּב֣וּל עוֹלָ֑ם אֲשֶׁ֖ר עָשׂ֣וּ אֲבוֹתֶֽיךָ׃
*Al-tasseg gevul olam, asher asu avotekha.*
Do not remove the ancient boundary stone
that your ancestors set up.

**29**
חָזִ֡יתָ אִ֤ישׁ מָ֘הִ֤יר בִּמְלַאכְתּ֗וֹ לִֽפְנֵי־מְלָכִ֥ים יִתְיַצָּ֑ב בַּל־יִ֝תְיַצֵּ֗ב לִפְנֵ֥י חֲשֻׁכִּֽים׃
*Chazita ish mahir bi-melakhto, lifnei-melakhim yityatzav, bal-yityatzev lifnei cheshukkim.*
See a man skilled at his work —
he shall attend upon kings;
he shall not attend upon the lowly.
