# Proverbs 26

← [[theproverbs/Proverbs_25|Proverbs 25]]  ·  [[theproverbs/Proverbs_27|Proverbs 27]] →

---

**1**
כַּשֶּׁ֤לֶג בַּקַּ֗יִץ וְכַמָּטָ֥ר בַּקָּצִ֑יר כֵּ֤ן לֹֽא־נָאוֶ֖ה לִכְסִ֣יל כָּבֽוֹד׃
*Ka-sheleg ba-qayitz ve-ka-matar ba-qatzir, ken lo-na'aveh li-khesil kavod.*
Like snow in summer and rain at harvesttime, so honor is not fitting for a dullard.

**2**
כַּצִּפּ֣וֹר לָ֭נוּד כַּדְּר֣וֹר לָע֑וּף כֵּ֥ן קִֽלְלַ֥ת חִ֝נָּ֗ם לוֹ תָבֹֽא׃
*Ka-tzippor la-nud ka-deror la-uf, ken qilelat chinnam lo tavo.*
As a sparrow must flit and a swallow fly, so a gratuitous curse must backfire.

**3**
שׁ֣וֹט לַ֭סּוּס מֶ֣תֶג לַחֲמ֑וֹר וְ֝שֵׁ֗בֶט לְגֵ֣ו כְּסִילִֽים׃
*Shot la-sus meteg la-chamor, ve-shevet le-gev kesilim.*
A whip for a horse and a bridle for a donkey, and a rod for the back of dullards.

**4**
אַל־תַּ֣עַן כְּ֭סִיל כְּאִוַּלְתּ֑וֹ פֶּֽן־תִּשְׁוֶה־לּ֥וֹ גַם־אָֽתָּה׃
*Al-ta'an kesil ke-ivvalto, pen-tishveh-lo gam-attah.*
Do not answer a dullard in accord with his folly, else you will become like him.

**5**
עֲנֵ֣ה כְ֭סִיל כְּאִוַּלְתּ֑וֹ פֶּן־יִהְיֶ֖ה חָכָ֣ם בְּעֵינָֽיו׃
*Aneh khesil ke-ivvalto, pen-yihyeh chakham be-einav.*
Answer a dullard in accord with his folly, else he will think himself wise.

**6**
מְקַצֶּ֣ה רַ֭גְלַיִם חָמָ֣ס שֹׁתֶ֑ה שֹׁלֵ֖חַ דְּבָרִ֣ים בְּיַד־כְּסִֽיל׃
*Meqatzeh raglayim chamas shoteh, sholeach devarim be-yad-kesil.*
One who sends a message by a dullard will wear out legs and must put up with lawlessness.

**7**
דַּלְי֣וּ שֹׁ֭קַיִם מִפִּסֵּ֑חַ וּ֝מָשָׁ֗ל בְּפִ֣י כְסִילִֽים׃
*Dalyu shoqayim mi-pisseiach, u-mashal be-fi khesilim.*
As legs hang limp on a cripple, so is a proverb in the mouth of dullards.

**8**
כִּצְר֣וֹר אֶ֭בֶן בְּמַרְגֵּמָ֑ה כֵּן־נוֹתֵ֖ן לִכְסִ֣יל כָּבֽוֹד׃
*Ki-tzror even be-marggemah, ken-noten li-khesil kavod.*
Like a pebble in a sling, so is paying honor to a dullard.

**9**
ח֭וֹחַ עָלָ֣ה בְיַד־שִׁכּ֑וֹר וּ֝מָשָׁ֗ל בְּפִ֣י כְסִילִֽים׃
*Choach alah ve-yad-shikkor, u-mashal be-fi khesilim.*
As a thorn comes to the hand of a drunkard, so a proverb to the mouth of a dullard.

**10**
רַ֥ב מְחוֹלֵֽל־כֹּ֑ל וְשֹׂכֵ֥ר כְּ֝סִ֗יל וְשֹׂכֵ֥ר עֹבְרִֽים׃
*Rav mecholel-kol, ve-sokher kesil ve-sokher ovrim.*
A master can produce anything, but anyone who hires a dullard is as one who hires transients.

**11**
כְּ֭כֶלֶב שָׁ֣ב עַל־קֵא֑וֹ כְּ֝סִ֗יל שׁוֹנֶ֥ה בְאִוַּלְתּֽוֹ׃
*Ke-khelev shav al-qe'o, kesil shoneh ve-ivvalto.*
As a dog returns to its vomit, so a dullard repeats his folly.

**12**
רָאִ֗יתָ אִ֭ישׁ חָכָ֣ם בְּעֵינָ֑יו תִּקְוָ֖ה לִכְסִ֣יל מִמֶּֽנּוּ׃
*Ra'ita ish chakham be-einav, tiqvah li-khesil mimmennu.*
If you see a man who thinks himself wise, there is more hope for a dullard than for him.

**13**
אָמַ֣ר עָ֭צֵל שַׁ֣חַל בַּדָּ֑רֶךְ אֲ֝רִ֗י בֵּ֣ין הָרְחֹבֽוֹת׃
*Amar atzel, shachal ba-derekh, ari bein ha-rechovot.*
The sluggard says, "There's a cub on the road, a lion in the squares."

**14**
הַ֭דֶּלֶת תִּסּ֣וֹב עַל־צִירָ֑הּ וְ֝עָצֵ֗ל עַל־מִטָּתֽוֹ׃
*Ha-delet tissov al-tziyrah, ve-atzel al-mittato.*
The door turns upon the hinge, and the sluggard upon the bed.

**15**
טָ֘מַ֤ן עָצֵ֣ל יָ֭דוֹ בַּצַּלָּ֑חַת נִ֝לְאָ֗ה לַהֲשִׁיבָ֥הּ אֶל־פִּֽיו׃
*Taman atzel yado ba-tzallachat, nil'ah la-hashivah el-piv.*
The sluggard buries a hand in the bowl, and will not even bring it to the mouth.

**16**
חָכָ֣ם עָצֵ֣ל בְּעֵינָ֑יו מִ֝שִּׁבְעָ֗ה מְשִׁ֣יבֵי טָֽעַם׃
*Chakham atzel be-einav, mi-shiv'ah meshivei ta'am.*
In self-regard, the sluggard is wiser than seven who give good advice.

**17**
מַחֲזִ֥יק בְּאׇזְנֵי־כָ֑לֶב עֹבֵ֥ר מִ֝תְעַבֵּ֗ר עַל־רִ֥יב לֹּא־לֽוֹ׃
*Machaziq be-oznei-khelev, over mit'abber al-riv lo-lo.*
A passerby who gets embroiled in someone else's quarrel is like one who seizes a dog by its ears.

**18**
כְּֽ֭מִתְלַהְלֵהַּ הַיֹּרֶ֥ה זִקִּ֗ים חִצִּ֥ים וָמָֽוֶת׃
*Ke-mitlahleiha ha-yoreh ziqqim, chitzim va-mavet.*
Like a lunatic scattering deadly firebrands, arrows,

**19**
כֵּֽן־אִ֭ישׁ רִמָּ֣ה אֶת־רֵעֵ֑הוּ וְ֝אָמַ֗ר הֲֽלֹא־מְשַׂחֵ֥ק אָֽנִי׃
*Ken-ish rimmah et-re'eihu, ve-amar halo-mesacheq ani.*
Is the man who cheats his fellow and says, "I was only joking."

**20**
בְּאֶ֣פֶס עֵ֭צִים תִּכְבֶּה־אֵ֑שׁ וּבְאֵ֥ין נִ֝רְגָּ֗ן יִשְׁתֹּ֥ק מָדֽוֹן׃
*Be-efes etzim tikbeh-esh, u-ve-ein nirgan yishtok madon.*
For lack of wood a fire goes out, and without a grumbler contention is stilled.

**21**
פֶּחָ֣ם לְ֭גֶחָלִים וְעֵצִ֣ים לְאֵ֑שׁ וְאִ֥ישׁ מִ֝דְיָנִ֗ים לְחַרְחַר־רִֽיב׃
*Pecham le-gechalim ve-etzim le-esh, ve-ish midyanim le-charchar-riv.*
Charcoal for embers and wood for a fire, and a contentious man for kindling strife.

**22**
דִּבְרֵ֣י נִ֭רְגָּן כְּמִֽתְלַהֲמִ֑ים וְ֝הֵ֗ם יָרְד֥וּ חַדְרֵי־בָֽטֶן׃
*Divrei nirgan ke-mitlahamim, ve-hem yaredu chadrei-vaten.*
The words of a grumbler are bruising; they penetrate one's inmost parts.

**23**
כֶּ֣סֶף סִ֭יגִים מְצֻפֶּ֣ה עַל־חָ֑רֶשׂ שְׂפָתַ֖יִם דֹּלְקִ֣ים וְלֶב־רָֽע׃
*Kesef sigim metzuppeh al-chares, sefatayim dolqim ve-lev-ra.*
Base silver laid over earthenware are ardent lips with an evil mind.

**24**
בִּ֭שְׂפָתָו יִנָּכֵ֣ר שׂוֹנֵ֑א וּ֝בְקִרְבּ֗וֹ יָשִׁ֥ית מִרְמָֽה׃
*Bi-sfatav yinnaker soneh, u-ve-qirbo yashit mirmah.*
An enemy dissembles with his speech; inwardly he harbors deceit.

**25**
כִּֽי־יְחַנֵּ֣ן ק֭וֹלוֹ אַל־תַּאֲמֶן־בּ֑וֹ כִּ֤י שֶׁ֖בַע תּוֹעֵב֣וֹת בְּלִבּֽוֹ׃
*Ki-yechannen qolo al-ta'amen-bo, ki sheva to'evot be-libbo.*
Though he be fair-spoken do not trust him, for seven abominations are in his mind.

**26**
תִּכַּסֶּ֣ה שִׂ֭נְאָה בְּמַשָּׁא֑וֹן תִּגָּלֶ֖ה רָעָת֣וֹ בְקָהָֽל׃
*Tikkasseh sin'ah be-massa'on, tiggaleh ra'ato ve-qahal.*
His hatred may be concealed by dissimulation, but his evil will be exposed to public view.

**27**
כֹּֽרֶה־שַּׁ֭חַת בָּ֣הּ יִפּ֑וֹל וְג֥וֹלֵֽל אֶ֝֗בֶן אֵלָ֥יו תָּשֽׁוּב׃
*Koreh-shachat bah yippol, ve-golel even elav tashuv.*
One who digs a pit will fall in it, and a stone will roll back upon the one who is rolling it.

**28**
לְֽשׁוֹן־שֶׁ֭קֶר יִשְׂנָ֣א דַכָּ֑יו וּפֶ֥ה חָ֝לָ֗ק יַעֲשֶׂ֥ה מִדְחֶֽה׃
*Leshon-sheqer yisna dakkav, u-feh chalaq ya'aseh midcheh.*
A lying tongue hates those crushed by it; smooth speech throws one down.
