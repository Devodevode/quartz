## Verses 1–10: Trust in GOD

**1**
בְּ֭נִי תּוֹרָתִ֣י אַל־תִּשְׁכָּ֑ח וּ֝מִצְוֺתַ֗י יִצֹּ֥ר לִבֶּֽךָ׃
*Beni torati al-tishkach, u-mitzvotai yitztor libbkha.*
My son, do not forget my teaching, but let your mind retain my commandments;

**2**
כִּ֤י אֹ֣רֶךְ יָ֭מִים וּשְׁנ֣וֹת חַיִּ֑ים וְ֝שָׁל֗וֹם יוֹסִ֥יפוּ לָֽךְ׃
*Ki orekh yamim u-shenot chayyim, ve-shalom yosifu lakh.*
For they will bestow on you length of days, years of life and well-being.

**3**
חֶ֥סֶד וֶאֱמֶ֗ת אַֽל־יַ֫עַזְבֻ֥ךָ קׇשְׁרֵ֥ם עַל־גַּרְגְּרוֹתֶ֑יךָ כׇּ֝תְבֵ֗ם עַל־ל֥וּחַ לִבֶּֽךָ׃
*Chesed ve-emet al-ya'azvukha, qoshrem al-gargerotekha, kotevem al-lu'ach libbkha.*
Let fidelity and steadfastness not leave you; bind them about your throat, write them on the tablet of your mind,

**4**
וּמְצָא־חֵ֥ן וְשֵֽׂכֶל־ט֑וֹב בְּעֵינֵ֖י אֱלֹהִ֣ים וְאָדָֽם׃
*U-metza-chen ve-sekhel-tov, be-einei Elohim ve-adam.*
And you will find favor and approbation in the eyes of God and human beings.

**5**
בְּטַ֣ח אֶל־יְ֭הֹוָה בְּכׇל־לִבֶּ֑ךָ וְאֶל־בִּ֥֝ינָתְךָ֗ אַל־תִּשָּׁעֵֽן׃
*Betach el-Adonai be-khol-libbkha, ve-el-binatekha al-tisha'en.*
Trust in GOD with all your heart, and do not rely on your own understanding.

**6**
בְּכׇל־דְּרָכֶ֥יךָ דָעֵ֑הוּ וְ֝ה֗וּא יְיַשֵּׁ֥ר אֹֽרְחֹתֶֽיךָ׃
*Be-khol-derakhekha da'ehu, ve-hu yeyasher orchotekha.*
In all your ways acknowledge [God], and your paths will be made smooth.

**7**
אַל־תְּהִ֣י חָכָ֣ם בְּעֵינֶ֑יךָ יְרָ֥א אֶת־יְ֝הֹוָ֗ה וְס֣וּר מֵרָֽע׃
*Al-tehi chakham be-einekha, yera et-Adonai ve-sur me-ra.*
Do not be wise in your own eyes; fear GOD and shun evil.

**8**
רִ֭פְאוּת תְּהִ֣י לְשׇׁרֶּ֑ךָ וְ֝שִׁקּ֗וּי לְעַצְמוֹתֶֽיךָ׃
*Rifu'ot tehi le-shorrekha, ve-shiqquy le-atzmotekha.*
It will be a cure for your body, a tonic for your bones.

**9**
כַּבֵּ֣ד אֶת־יְ֭הֹוָה מֵהוֹנֶ֑ךָ וּ֝מֵרֵאשִׁ֗ית כׇּל־תְּבוּאָתֶֽךָ׃
*Kabbed et-Adonai me-honekha, u-me-reshit kol-tevu'atekha.*
Honor GOD with your wealth, with the best of all your income,

**10**
וְיִמָּלְא֣וּ אֲסָמֶ֣יךָ שָׂבָ֑ע וְ֝תִיר֗וֹשׁ יְקָבֶ֥יךָ יִפְרֹֽצוּ׃
*Ve-yimmale'u asamekha sava, ve-tirosh yeqavekha yifrotzu.*
And your barns will be filled with grain, your vats will burst with new wine.

## Verses 11–20: The Value of Wisdom

**11**
מוּסַ֣ר יְ֭הֹוָה בְּנִ֣י אַל־תִּמְאָ֑ס וְאַל־תָּ֝קֹ֗ץ בְּתוֹכַחְתּֽוֹ׃
*Musar Adonai beni al-timas, ve-al-taqotz be-tokhacho.*
Do not reject GOD's discipline, my son; do not abhor such rebuke.

**12**
כִּ֤י אֶ֥ת־אֲשֶׁ֣ר יֶאֱהַ֣ב יְהֹוָ֣ה יוֹכִ֑יחַ וּ֝כְאָ֗ב אֶת־בֵּ֥ן יִרְצֶֽה׃
*Ki et-asher ye'ehav Adonai yokhiach, u-khe-av et-ben yirtze.*
For whom GOD loves, [God] rebukes, as a father the son whom he favors.

**13**
אַשְׁרֵ֣י אָ֭דָם מָצָ֣א חׇכְמָ֑ה וְ֝אָדָ֗ם יָפִ֥יק תְּבוּנָֽה׃
*Ashrei adam matza chokhmah, ve-adam yafiq tevunah.*
Happy is the one who finds wisdom, the one who attains understanding.

**14**
כִּ֤י ט֣וֹב סַ֭חְרָהּ מִסְּחַר־כָּ֑סֶף וּ֝מֵחָר֗וּץ תְּבוּאָתָֽהּ׃
*Ki tov sachrah mi-sechar-khesef, u-me-charutz tevu'atah.*
Her value in trade is better than silver, her yield, greater than gold.

**15**
יְקָ֣רָה הִ֭יא מִפְּנִינִ֑ים וְכׇל־חֲ֝פָצֶ֗יךָ לֹ֣א יִֽשְׁווּ־בָֽהּ׃
*Yeqarah hi mi-peninim, ve-khol-chafatzekha lo yishvu-vah.*
She is more precious than rubies; all of your goods cannot equal her.

**16**
אֹ֣רֶךְ יָ֭מִים בִּֽימִינָ֑הּ בִּ֝שְׂמֹאולָ֗הּ עֹ֣שֶׁר וְכָבֽוֹד׃
*Orekh yamim bi-yminah, bi-smolah osher ve-khavod.*
In her right hand is length of days, in her left, riches and honor.

**17**
דְּרָכֶ֥יהָ דַרְכֵי־נֹ֑עַם וְֽכׇל־נְתִ֖יבוֹתֶ֣יהָ שָׁלֽוֹם׃
*Derakheiha darkhei-no'am, ve-khol-netivoteha shalom.*
Her ways are pleasant ways, and all her paths, peaceful.

**18**
עֵץ־חַיִּ֣ים הִ֭יא לַמַּחֲזִיקִ֣ים בָּ֑הּ וְֽתֹמְכֶ֥יהָ מְאֻשָּֽׁר׃
*Etz-chayyim hi la-machaziqim bah, ve-tomekheiha me'ushar.*
She is a tree of life to those who grasp her, and whoever holds on to her is happy.

**19**
יְֽהֹוָ֗ה בְּחׇכְמָ֥ה יָסַד־אָ֑רֶץ כּוֹנֵ֥ן שָׁ֝מַ֗יִם בִּתְבוּנָֽה׃
*Adonai be-chokhmah yasad-aretz, konen shamayim bi-tevunah.*
GOD founded the earth by wisdom and established the heavens by understanding;

**20**
בְּ֭דַעְתּוֹ תְּהוֹמ֣וֹת נִבְקָ֑עוּ וּ֝שְׁחָקִ֗ים יִרְעֲפוּ־טָֽל׃
*Be-da'to tehomot nivqa'u, u-shechaqim yir'afu-tal.*
By God's knowledge the depths burst apart, and the skies distilled dew.

## Verses 21–35: Further Counsel

**21**
בְּ֭נִי אַל־יָלֻ֣זוּ מֵעֵינֶ֑יךָ נְצֹ֥ר תֻּ֝שִׁיָּ֗ה וּמְזִמָּֽה׃
*Beni al-yaluzu me-einekha, netzor tushiyyah u-mezimmah.*
My son, do not lose sight of them; hold on to resourcefulness and foresight.

**22**
וְיִֽהְי֣וּ חַיִּ֣ים לְנַפְשֶׁ֑ךָ וְ֝חֵ֗ן לְגַרְגְּרֹתֶֽיךָ׃
*Ve-yihyu chayyim le-nafshekha, ve-chen le-gargerotekha.*
They will give life to your spirit and grace to your throat.

**23**
אָ֤ז תֵּלֵ֣ךְ לָבֶ֣טַח דַּרְכֶּ֑ךָ וְ֝רַגְלְךָ֗ לֹ֣א תִגּֽוֹף׃
*Az telekh lavetach darkkha, ve-raglekha lo tiggof.*
Then you will go your way safely and not injure your feet.

**24**
אִם־תִּשְׁכַּ֥ב לֹֽא־תִפְחָ֑ד וְ֝שָׁכַבְתָּ֗ וְֽעָרְבָ֥ה שְׁנָתֶֽךָ׃
*Im-tishkav lo-tifchad, ve-shakhavta ve-arvah shenatekha.*
When you lie down you will be unafraid; you will lie down and your sleep will be sweet.

**25**
אַל־תִּ֭ירָא מִפַּ֣חַד פִּתְאֹ֑ם וּמִשֹּׁאַ֥ת רְ֝שָׁעִ֗ים כִּ֣י תָבֹֽא׃
*Al-tira mi-pachad pit'om, u-mi-sho'at resha'im ki tavo.*
You will not fear sudden terror or the disaster that comes upon the wicked,

**26**
כִּֽי־יְ֭הֹוָה יִֽהְיֶ֣ה בְכִסְלֶ֑ךָ וְשָׁמַ֖ר רַגְלְךָ֣ מִלָּֽכֶד׃
*Ki-Adonai yihyeh ve-khislekha, ve-shamar raglekha mi-llakhad.*
For GOD will be your trust and will keep your feet from being caught.

**27**
אַל־תִּמְנַע־ט֥וֹב מִבְּעָלָ֑יו בִּֽהְי֨וֹת לְאֵ֖ל יָדְךָ֣ לַעֲשֽׂוֹת׃
*Al-timna-tov mi-be'alav, bihyot le-el yadekha la-asot.*
Do not withhold good from those who deserve it when you have the power to do it [for them].

**28**
אַל־תֹּ֘אמַ֤ר לְרֵעֲךָ֨ לֵ֣ךְ וָ֭שׁוּב וּמָחָ֥ר אֶתֵּ֗ן וְיֵ֣שׁ אִתָּֽךְ׃
*Al-tomar le-re'akha lekh va-shuv u-machar etten, ve-yesh ittakh.*
Do not say to your fellow, "Come back again; I'll give it to you tomorrow," when you have it with you.

**29**
אַל־תַּחֲרֹ֣שׁ עַל־רֵעֲךָ֣ רָעָ֑ה וְהֽוּא־יוֹשֵׁ֖ב לָבֶ֣טַח אִתָּֽךְ׃
*Al-tachharosh al-re'akha ra'ah, ve-hu-yoshev lavetach ittakh.*
Do not devise harm against your fellow who lives trustfully with you.

**30**
אַל־תָּרִ֣יב עִם־אָדָ֣ם חִנָּ֑ם אִם־לֹ֖א גְמָלְךָ֣ רָעָֽה׃
*Al-tariv im-adam chinnam, im-lo gemalekha ra'ah.*
Do not quarrel with someone for no cause, when they have done you no harm.

**31**
אַל־תְּ֭קַנֵּא בְּאִ֣ישׁ חָמָ֑ס וְאַל־תִּ֝בְחַ֗ר בְּכׇל־דְּרָכָֽיו׃
*Al-teqanne be-ish chamas, ve-al-tivchar be-khol-derakhav.*
Do not envy a lawless man, or choose any of his ways;

**32**
כִּ֤י תוֹעֲבַ֣ת יְהֹוָ֣ה נָל֑וֹז וְֽאֶת־יְשָׁרִ֥ים סוֹדֽוֹ׃
*Ki to'avat Adonai naloz, ve-et-yesharim sodo.*
For the one who goes astray is an abomination to GOD, but the straightforward are intimates.

**33**
מְאֵרַ֣ת יְ֭הֹוָה בְּבֵ֣ית רָשָׁ֑ע וּנְוֵ֖ה צַדִּיקִ֣ים יְבָרֵֽךְ׃
*Me'erat Adonai be-veit rasha, u-neveh tzaddiqim yevarekh.*
GOD's curse is on the house of the evildoer, but the abode of the righteous is blessed.

**34**
אִם־לַלֵּצִ֥ים הֽוּא־יָלִ֑יץ וְ֝לַעֲנָוִ֗ים יִתֶּן־חֵֽן׃
*Im-la-letzim hu-yahlitz, ve-la-anavim yitten-chen.*
At scoffers [God] scoffs, but the lowly are shown grace.

**35**
כָּ֭בוֹד חֲכָמִ֣ים יִנְחָ֑לוּ וּ֝כְסִילִ֗ים מֵרִ֥ים קָלֽוֹן׃
*Kavod chakhamim yinchalu, u-khesilim merim qalon.*
The wise shall obtain honor, but dullards get disgrace as their portion.
