## Verses 1–9: The Words of Lemuel

**1**
דִּ֭בְרֵי לְמוּאֵ֣ל מֶ֑לֶךְ מַ֝שָּׂ֗א אֲֽשֶׁר־יִסְּרַ֥תּוּ אִמּֽוֹ׃
*Divrei Lemuel melekh, massa, asher-yisserattu immo.*
The words of Lemuel, king of Massa, with which his mother admonished him.

**2**
מַה־בְּ֭רִי וּמַֽה־בַּר־בִּטְנִ֑י וּ֝מֶ֗ה בַּר־נְדָרָֽי׃
*Mah-berri u-mah-bar-bitni, u-meh bar-nedarai.*
No, my son! No, O son of my womb! No, O son of my vows!

**3**
אַל־תִּתֵּ֣ן לַנָּשִׁ֣ים חֵילֶ֑ךָ וּ֝דְרָכֶ֗יךָ לַֽמְח֥וֹת מְלָכִֽין׃
*Al-titten la-nashim cheilekha, u-derakhekha la-mechot melakhin.*
Do not give your strength to women, your vigor, to those who destroy kings.

**4**
אַ֤ל לַֽמְלָכִ֨ים לְֽמוֹאֵ֗ל אַ֣ל לַֽמְלָכִ֣ים שְׁתוֹ־יָ֑יִן וּ֝לְרוֹזְנִ֗ים אֵ֣י שֵׁכָֽר׃
*Al la-melakhim, Lemuel, al la-melakhim sheto-yayin, u-le-rozenim ei shekhar.*
Wine is not for kings, O Lemuel; not for kings to drink, nor any strong drink for princes,

**5**
פֶּן־יִ֭שְׁתֶּה וְיִשְׁכַּ֣ח מְחֻקָּ֑ק וִ֝ישַׁנֶּ֗ה דִּ֣ין כׇּל־בְּנֵי־עֹֽנִי׃
*Pen-yishteh ve-yishkach mechugqaq, vi-yeshanneh din kol-benei-oni.*
Lest they drink and forget what has been ordained, and infringe on the rights of the poor.

**6**
תְּנוּ־שֵׁכָ֥ר לְאוֹבֵ֑ד וְ֝יַ֗יִן לְמָ֣רֵי נָֽפֶשׁ׃
*Tenu-shekhar le-oved, ve-yayin le-marei nafesh.*
Give strong drink to the hapless and wine to the embittered.

**7**
יִ֭שְׁתֶּה וְיִשְׁכַּ֣ח רִישׁ֑וֹ וַ֝עֲמָל֗וֹ לֹ֣א יִזְכׇּר־עֽוֹד׃
*Yishteh ve-yishkach risho, va-amalo lo yizzkor-od.*
Let them drink and forget their poverty, and put their troubles out of mind.

**8**
פְּתַח־פִּ֥יךָ לְאִלֵּ֑ם אֶל־דִּ֝֗ין כׇּל־בְּנֵ֥י חֲלֽוֹף׃
*Petach-pikha le-illem, el-din kol-benei chalof.*
Speak up for the dumb, for the rights of all the unfortunate.

**9**
פְּתַח־פִּ֥יךָ שְׁפׇט־צֶ֑דֶק וְ֝דִ֗ין עָנִ֥י וְאֶבְיֽוֹן׃
*Petach-pikha shefot-tzedeq, ve-din ani ve-evyon.*
Speak up, judge righteously, champion the poor and the needy.

## Verses 10–31: The Woman of Valor

**10**
אֵֽשֶׁת־חַ֭יִל מִ֣י יִמְצָ֑א וְרָחֹ֖ק מִפְּנִינִ֣ים מִכְרָֽהּ׃
*Eshet-chayil mi yimtza, ve-rachok mi-peninim mikhrah.*
How precious is a woman of substance! Her worth is far beyond that of rubies.

**11**
בָּ֣טַח בָּ֭הּ לֵ֣ב בַּעְלָ֑הּ וְ֝שָׁלָ֗ל לֹ֣א יֶחְסָֽר׃
*Vatach bah lev ba'lah, ve-shalal lo yechsar.*
Her husband puts his confidence in her, and lacks no good thing.

**12**
גְּמָלַ֣תְהוּ ט֣וֹב וְלֹא־רָ֑ע כֹּ֝֗ל יְמֵ֣י חַיֶּֽיהָ׃
*Gemalatthu tov ve-lo-ra, kol yemei chayyeiha.*
She is good to him, never bad, all the days of her life.

**13**
דָּ֭רְשָׁה צֶ֣מֶר וּפִשְׁתִּ֑ים וַ֝תַּ֗עַשׂ בְּחֵ֣פֶץ כַּפֶּֽיהָ׃
*Darshah tzemer u-fishtim, va-ta'as be-chefetz kappeha.*
She looks for wool and flax, and sets her hand to them with a will.

**14**
הָ֭יְתָה כׇּאֳנִיּ֣וֹת סוֹחֵ֑ר מִ֝מֶּרְחָ֗ק תָּבִ֥יא לַחְמָֽהּ׃
*Haytah ke-oniyyot socher, mi-merchaq tavi lachmah.*
She is like a merchant fleet, bringing her food from afar.

**15**
וַתָּ֤קׇם בְּע֬וֹד לַ֗יְלָה וַתִּתֵּ֣ן טֶ֣רֶף לְבֵיתָ֑הּ וְ֝חֹ֗ק לְנַעֲרֹתֶֽיהָ׃
*Va-taqom be-od laylah, va-titten teref le-veitah, ve-choq le-na'aroteiha.*
She rises while it is still night, and supplies provisions for her household, the daily fare of her maids.

**16**
זָמְמָ֣ה שָׂ֭דֶה וַתִּקָּחֵ֑הוּ מִפְּרִ֥י כַ֝פֶּ֗יהָ נָ֣טְעָה כָּֽרֶם׃
*Zammemah sadeh va-tiqqacheihu, mi-peri kappeha nat'ah karem.*
She sets her mind on an estate and acquires it; she plants a vineyard by her own labors.

**17**
חָֽגְרָ֣ה בְע֣וֹז מׇתְנֶ֑יהָ וַ֝תְּאַמֵּ֗ץ זְרוֹעֹתֶֽיהָ׃
*Chaggorah ve-oz matneiha, va-te'ammetz zero'oteiha.*
She girds herself with strength, and performs her tasks with vigor.

**18**
טָ֭עֲמָה כִּי־ט֣וֹב סַחְרָ֑הּ לֹא־יִכְבֶּ֖ה בַלַּ֣יְלָה נֵרָֽהּ׃
*Ta'amah ki-tov sachrah, lo-yikhbeh ba-laylah nerah.*
She sees that her business thrives; her lamp never goes out at night.

**19**
יָ֭דֶיהָ שִׁלְּחָ֣ה בַכִּישׁ֑וֹר וְ֝כַפֶּ֗יהָ תָּ֣מְכוּ פָֽלֶךְ׃
*Yadeiha shillechah va-kishor, ve-chappeha tamkhu falekh.*
She sets her hand to the distaff; her fingers work the spindle.

**20**
כַּ֭פָּהּ פָּֽרְשָׂ֣ה לֶעָנִ֑י וְ֝יָדֶ֗יהָ שִׁלְּחָ֥ה לָאֶבְיֽוֹן׃
*Kappah parsah le-ani, ve-yadeiha shillechah la-evyon.*
She gives generously to the poor; her hands are stretched out to the needy.

**21**
לֹא־תִירָ֣א לְבֵיתָ֣הּ מִשָּׁ֑לֶג כִּ֥י כׇל־בֵּ֝יתָ֗הּ לָבֻ֥שׁ שָׁנִֽים׃
*Lo-tira le-veitah mi-shaleg, ki khol-beitah lavush shanim.*
She is not worried for her household because of snow, for her whole household is dressed in crimson.

**22**
מַרְבַדִּ֥ים עָֽשְׂתָה־לָּ֑הּ שֵׁ֖שׁ וְאַרְגָּמָ֣ן לְבוּשָֽׁהּ׃
*Marvaddim astah-lah, shesh ve-argaman levushah.*
She makes covers for herself; her clothing is linen and purple.

**23**
נוֹדָ֣ע בַּשְּׁעָרִ֣ים בַּעְלָ֑הּ בְּ֝שִׁבְתּ֗וֹ עִם־זִקְנֵי־אָֽרֶץ׃
*Noda ba-she'arim ba'lah, be-shivto im-ziqnei-aretz.*
Her husband is prominent in the gates, as he sits among the elders of the land.

**24**
סָדִ֣ין עָ֭שְׂתָה וַתִּמְכֹּ֑ר וַ֝חֲג֗וֹר נָתְנָ֥ה לַֽכְּנַעֲנִֽי׃
*Sadin astah va-timkor, va-chagor natenah la-kena'ani.*
She makes cloth and sells it, and offers a girdle to the merchant.

**25**
עֹז־וְהָדָ֥ר לְבוּשָׁ֑הּ וַ֝תִּשְׂחַ֗ק לְי֣וֹם אַחֲרֽוֹן׃
*Oz-ve-hadar levushah, va-tischaq le-yom acharon.*
She is clothed with strength and splendor; she looks to the future cheerfully.

**26**
פִּ֭יהָ פָּתְחָ֣ה בְחׇכְמָ֑ה וְת֥וֹרַת חֶ֝֗סֶד עַל־לְשׁוֹנָֽהּ׃
*Piha patchah ve-chokhmah, ve-torat chesed al-leshonah.*
Her mouth is full of wisdom, her tongue with kindly teaching.

**27**
צ֭וֹפִיָּה הֲלִיכ֣וֹת בֵּיתָ֑הּ וְלֶ֥חֶם עַ֝צְל֗וּת לֹ֣א תֹאכֵֽל׃
*Tzofiyyah halikot beitah, ve-lechem atzelut lo tokhal.*
She oversees the activities of her household and never eats the bread of idleness.

**28**
קָ֣מוּ בָ֭נֶיהָ וַֽיְאַשְּׁר֑וּהָ בַּ֝עְלָ֗הּ וַֽיְהַלְלָֽהּ׃
*Qamu vaneiha va-ye'asheruha, ba'lah va-yehallelah.*
Her children declare her happy; her husband praises her,

**29**
רַבּ֣וֹת בָּ֭נוֹת עָ֣שׂוּ חָ֑יִל וְ֝אַ֗תְּ עָלִ֥ית עַל־כֻּלָּֽנָה׃
*Rabbot banot asu chayil, ve-att alit al-kullanah.*
"Many women have done well, but you surpass them all."

**30**
שֶׁ֣קֶר הַ֭חֵן וְהֶ֣בֶל הַיֹּ֑פִי אִשָּׁ֥ה יִרְאַת־יְ֝הֹוָ֗ה הִ֣יא תִתְהַלָּֽל׃
*Sheqer ha-chen ve-hevel ha-yofi, ishah yir'at-Adonai hi tithalal.*
Grace is deceptive, beauty is illusory; it is for her fear of GOD that a woman is to be praised.

**31**
תְּנוּ־לָ֭הּ מִפְּרִ֣י יָדֶ֑יהָ וִיהַלְל֖וּהָ בַשְּׁעָרִ֣ים מַֽעֲשֶֽׂיהָ׃
*Tenu-lah mi-peri yadeiha, vi-yehalleluha va-she'arim ma'aseiha.*
Give her of the fruit of her hands, and let her works praise her in the gates.
