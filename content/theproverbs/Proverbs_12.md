## Verses 1–28: Wisdom, Righteousness, and Their Fruits

**1**
אֹהֵ֣ב מוּסָ֣ר אֹהֵ֣ב דָּ֑עַת וְשֹׂנֵ֥א תוֹכַ֝חַ֗ת בָּֽעַר׃
*Ohev musar ohev da'at, ve-sonei tokhechat ba'ar.*
Whoever loves discipline loves knowledge; one who spurns reproof is a brute.

**2**
ט֗וֹב יָפִ֣יק רָצ֣וֹן מֵיְהֹוָ֑ה וְאִ֖ישׁ מְזִמּ֣וֹת יַרְשִֽׁיעַ׃
*Tov yafiq ratzon me-Adonai, ve-ish mezimmot yarshia.*
A good man earns GOD's favor, but a man of schemes GOD condemns.

**3**
לֹא־יִכּ֣וֹן אָ֭דָם בְּרֶ֑שַׁע וְשֹׁ֥רֶשׁ צַ֝דִּיקִ֗ים בַּל־יִמּֽוֹט׃
*Lo-yikkon adam be-resha, ve-shoresh tzaddiqim bal-yimmot.*
No man can be established through wickedness, but the root of the righteous will not be shaken.

**4**
אֵֽשֶׁת־חַ֭יִל עֲטֶ֣רֶת בַּעְלָ֑הּ וּכְרָקָ֖ב בְּעַצְמוֹתָ֣יו מְבִישָֽׁה׃
*Eshet-chayil ateret ba'lah, u-khe-raqav be-atzmotav mevishah.*
A capable wife is a crown for her husband; a shameful one is like rot in his bones.

**5**
מַחְשְׁב֣וֹת צַדִּיקִ֣ים מִשְׁפָּ֑ט תַּחְבֻּל֖וֹת רְשָׁעִ֣ים מִרְמָֽה׃
*Machshevot tzaddiqim mishpat, tachbulot resha'im mirmah.*
The schemes of the righteous are just, but the plans of the wicked are deceitful.

**6**
דִּבְרֵ֣י רְ֭שָׁעִים אֱרָב־דָּ֑ם וּפִ֥י יְ֝שָׁרִ֗ים יַצִּילֵֽם׃
*Divrei resha'im erav-dam, u-fi yesharim yatzilem.*
The words of the wicked are a murderous ambush, but the mouth of the upright saves them.

**7**
הָפ֣וֹךְ רְ֭שָׁעִים וְאֵינָ֑ם וּבֵ֖ית צַדִּיקִ֣ים יַעֲמֹֽד׃
*Hafokh resha'im ve-einam, u-veit tzaddiqim ya'amod.*
Overturn the wicked and they are gone, but the house of the righteous stands firm.

**8**
לְפִי־שִׂכְל֥וֹ יְהֻלַּל־אִ֑ישׁ וְנַעֲוֵה־לֵ֝֗ב יִהְיֶ֥ה לָבֽוּז׃
*Le-fi-sikhlo yehullal-ish, ve-na'aveh-lev yihyeh la-vuz.*
A man is commended according to his intelligence, but a twisted mind is despised.

**9**
טֽוֹב־נִ֭קְלֶה וְעֶ֣בֶד ל֑וֹ מִמִּתְכַּבֵּ֝֗ד וַחֲסַר־לָֽחֶם׃
*Tov-niqle ve-eved lo, mi-mitkabed va-chasar-lachem.*
Better to be lightly esteemed and have a servant than to be pompous and lack bread.

**10**
יוֹדֵ֣עַ צַ֭דִּיק נֶ֣פֶשׁ בְּהֶמְתּ֑וֹ וְרַחֲמֵ֖י רְשָׁעִ֣ים אַכְזָרִֽי׃
*Yodea tzaddiq nefesh behemto, ve-rachamei resha'im akhzari.*
A righteous man knows the needs of his beast, but the compassion of the wicked is cruelty.

**11**
עֹבֵ֣ד אַ֭דְמָתוֹ יִשְׂבַּע־לָ֑חֶם וּמְרַדֵּ֥ף רֵ֝יקִ֗ים חֲסַר־לֵֽב׃
*Oved admato yisba-lechem, u-meraddef reqim chasar-lev.*
He who works his land will have his fill of food, but he who chases fantasies is devoid of sense.

**12**
חָמַ֣ד רָ֭שָׁע מְצ֣וֹד רָעִ֑ים וְשֹׁ֥רֶשׁ צַ֝דִּיקִ֗ים יִתֵּֽן׃
*Chamad rasha metzod ra'im, ve-shoresh tzaddiqim yitten.*
The wicked covet the spoils of evil men, but the root of the righteous gives forth.

**13**
בְּפֶ֣שַׁע שְׂ֭פָתַיִם מוֹקֵ֣שׁ רָ֑ע וַיֵּצֵ֖א מִצָּרָ֣ה צַדִּֽיק׃
*Be-fesha sefatayim moqesh ra, va-yetzei mi-tzarah tzaddiq.*
An evil man is trapped by his sinful speech, but a righteous man escapes from trouble.

**14**
מִפְּרִ֣י פִי־אִ֭ישׁ יִשְׂבַּ֣ע ט֑וֹב וּגְמ֥וּל יְ֝דֵי־אָדָ֗ם יָשׁ֥וּב לֽוֹ׃
*Mi-peri fi-ish yisba tov, u-gemul yedei-adam yashuv lo.*
A man is well satisfied with good things from the fruit of his speech, and the work of his hands comes back to him.

**15**
דֶּ֣רֶךְ אֱ֭וִיל יָשָׁ֣ר בְּעֵינָ֑יו וְשֹׁמֵ֥עַ לְ֝עֵצָ֗ה חָכָֽם׃
*Derekh evil yashar be-einav, ve-shome'a le-etzah chakham.*
The way of a fool is right in his own eyes, but a wise man heeds advice.

**16**
אֱוִיל֮ בַּיּ֪וֹם יִוָּדַ֫ע כַּעְס֥וֹ וְכֹסֶ֥ה קָל֗וֹן עָר֥וּם׃
*Evil ba-yom yivvada ka'so, ve-khose qalon arum.*
A fool's anger is known at once, but a prudent man conceals an insult.

**17**
יָפִ֣יחַ אֱמוּנָ֣ה יַגִּ֣יד צֶ֑דֶק וְעֵ֥ד שְׁ֝קָרִ֗ים מִרְמָֽה׃
*Yafiach emunah yaggid tzedeq, ve-ed sheqarim mirmah.*
A truthful witness tells what is right, but a false witness tells lies.

**18**
יֵ֣שׁ בּ֭וֹטֶה כְּמַדְקְר֣וֹת חָ֑רֶב וּלְשׁ֥וֹן חֲ֝כָמִ֗ים מַרְפֵּֽא׃
*Yesh boteh ke-madqerot charev, u-leshon chakhamim marpe.*
There is one who speaks rashly like the thrusts of a sword, but the tongue of the wise brings healing.

**19**
שְׂפַת־אֱמֶ֥ת תִּכּ֗וֹן לָ֫עַ֥ד וְעַד־אַרְגִּ֗יעָה לְשׁ֣וֹן שָֽׁקֶר׃
*Sefat-emet tikkon la-ad, ve-ad-argi'ah leshon shaqer.*
Truthful speech stands firm forever, but a lying tongue is but for a moment.

**20**
מִרְמָ֥ה בְּלֶב־חֹרְשֵׁ֣י רָ֑ע וּלְיֹעֲצֵ֖י שָׁל֣וֹם שִׂמְחָֽה׃
*Mirmah be-lev-chorshei ra, u-le-yo'atzei shalom simchah.*
Deceit is in the heart of those who plot evil, but those who counsel peace have joy.

**21**
לֹא־יְאֻנֶּ֣ה לַצַּדִּ֣יק כׇּל־אָ֑וֶן וְרָשָׁעִ֥ים מָ֝לְא֗וּ רָֽע׃
*Lo-ye'unneh la-tzaddiq kol-aven, ve-resha'im male'u ra.*
No harm befalls the righteous, but the wicked are filled with trouble.

**22**
תּוֹעֲבַ֣ת יְ֭הֹוָה שִׂפְתֵי־שָׁ֑קֶר וְעֹשֵׂ֥י אֱ֝מוּנָ֗ה רְצוֹנֽוֹ׃
*To'avat Adonai siftei-shaqer, ve-osei emunah retzona.*
Lying speech is an abomination to GOD, but those who act faithfully are his delight.

**23**
אָדָ֣ם עָ֭רוּם כֹּסֶ֣ה דָּ֑עַת וְלֵ֥ב כְּ֝סִילִ֗ים יִקְרָ֥א אִוֶּֽלֶת׃
*Adam arum kosse da'at, ve-lev kesilim yiqra ivvelet.*
A prudent man conceals knowledge, but the heart of dullards cries out folly.

**24**
יַד־חָר֥וּצִים תִּמְשׁ֑וֹל וּרְמִיָּ֝֗ה תִּהְיֶ֥ה לָמַֽס׃
*Yad-charuzim timsbol, u-remiyyah tihyeh la-mas.*
The diligent hand will rule, while the negligent will be put to forced labor.

**25**
דְּאָגָ֣ה בְלֶב־אִ֣ישׁ יַשְׁחֶ֑נָּה וְדָבָ֥ר ט֝֗וֹב יְשַׂמְּחֶֽנָּה׃
*De'agah ve-lev-ish yashchennah, ve-davar tov yesammechennah.*
Anxiety in a man's heart weighs it down, but a kind word gives it joy.

**26**
יָתֵ֣ר מֵ֭רֵעֵהוּ צַדִּ֑יק וְדֶ֖רֶךְ רְשָׁעִ֣ים תַּתְעֵֽם׃
*Yater me-re'ehu tzaddiq, ve-derekh resha'im tat'em.*
A righteous man has a care for the life of his fellow, but the path of the wicked leads them astray.

**27**
לֹא־יַחֲרֹ֣ךְ רְ֭מִיָּה צֵיד֑וֹ וְהוֹן־אָדָ֥ם יָ֝קָ֗ר חָרֽוּץ׃
*Lo-yacharokh remiyyah tzeido, ve-hon-adam yaqar charutz.*
A negligent man does not roast his game, but a diligent man has precious wealth.

**28**
בְּאֹֽרַח־צְדָקָ֥ה חַיִּ֑ים וְדֶ֖רֶךְ נְתִיבָ֣ה אַל־מָֽוֶת׃
*Be-orach-tzedaqah chayyim, ve-derekh netivah al-mavet.*
On the path of righteousness is life, and the way of its path leads to no death.
