# Proverbs 29

← [[theproverbs/Proverbs_28|Proverbs 28]]  ·  [[theproverbs/Proverbs_30|Proverbs 30]] →

---

**1**
אִ֣ישׁ תּ֭וֹכָחוֹת מַקְשֶׁה־עֹ֑רֶף פֶּ֥תַע יִ֝שָּׁבֵ֗ר וְאֵ֣ין מַרְפֵּֽא׃
*Ish tokhachot maqsheh-oref, peta yishshaver ve-ein marpe.*
One oft reproved may become stiffnecked — but then will be suddenly broken beyond repair.

**2**
בִּרְב֣וֹת צַ֭דִּיקִים יִשְׂמַ֣ח הָעָ֑ם וּבִמְשֹׁ֥ל רָ֝שָׁ֗ע יֵאָ֥נַֽח עָֽם׃
*Bi-revot tzaddiqim yismach ha-am, u-vi-msol rasha ye'anach am.*
When the righteous become great the people rejoice, but when the wicked dominate the people groan.

**3**
אִישׁ־אֹהֵ֣ב חׇ֭כְמָה יְשַׂמַּ֣ח אָבִ֑יו וְרֹעֶ֥ה ז֝וֹנ֗וֹת יְאַבֶּד־הֽוֹן׃
*Ish-ohev chokhmah yesammach aviv, ve-ro'eh zonot ye'abbed-hon.*
A man who loves wisdom brings joy to his father, but he who keeps company with prostitutes will lose his wealth.

**4**
מֶ֗לֶךְ בְּ֭מִשְׁפָּט יַעֲמִ֣יד אָ֑רֶץ וְאִ֖ישׁ תְּרוּמ֣וֹת יֶהֶרְסֶֽנָּה׃
*Melekh be-mishpat ya'amid aretz, ve-ish terumot yehersenah.*
By justice a king sustains the land, but a fraudulent man tears it down.

**5**
גֶּ֭בֶר מַחֲלִ֣יק עַל־רֵעֵ֑הוּ רֶ֝֗שֶׁת פּוֹרֵ֥שׂ עַל־פְּעָמָֽיו׃
*Gever machaliq al-re'eihu, reshet pores al-pe'amav.*
A man who flatters his fellow spreads a net for his feet.

**6**
בְּפֶ֤שַֽׁע אִ֣ישׁ רָ֣ע מוֹקֵ֑שׁ וְ֝צַדִּ֗יק יָר֥וּן וְשָׂמֵֽחַ׃
*Be-fesha ish ra moqesh, ve-tzaddiq yarun ve-samei'ach.*
An evil man's offenses are a trap for himself, but a righteous person sings out joyously.

**7**
יֹדֵ֣עַ צַ֭דִּיק דִּ֣ין דַּלִּ֑ים רָ֝שָׁ֗ע לֹא־יָבִ֥ין דָּֽעַת׃
*Yode'a tzaddiq din dallim, rasha lo-yavin da'at.*
The righteous are concerned with the cause of the wretched; the wicked cannot understand such concern.

**8**
אַנְשֵׁ֣י לָ֭צוֹן יָפִ֣יחוּ קִרְיָ֑ה וַ֝חֲכָמִ֗ים יָשִׁ֥יבוּ אָֽף׃
*Anshei latzon yafichu qiryah, va-chakhamim yashivu af.*
Scoffers inflame a city, but the wise allay anger.

**9**
אִֽישׁ־חָכָ֗ם נִ֭שְׁפָּט אֶת־אִ֣ישׁ אֱוִ֑יל וְרָגַ֥ז וְ֝שָׂחַ֗ק וְאֵ֣ין נָֽחַת׃
*Ish-chakham nishpat et-ish evil, ve-ragaz ve-sachaq ve-ein nachat.*
When someone who is wise enters into litigation with a fool there is ranting and ridicule, but no satisfaction.

**10**
אַנְשֵׁ֣י דָ֭מִים יִשְׂנְאוּ־תָ֑ם וִ֝ישָׁרִ֗ים יְבַקְשׁ֥וּ נַפְשֽׁוֹ׃
*Anshei damim yisne'u-tam, vi-yesharim yevaqshu nafsho.*
Those who are bloodthirsty detest the blameless, but the upright seek them out.

**11**
כׇּל־ר֭וּחוֹ יוֹצִ֣יא כְסִ֑יל וְ֝חָכָ֗ם בְּאָח֥וֹר יְשַׁבְּחֶֽנָּה׃
*Kol-rucho yotzi khesil, ve-chakham be-achor yeshabbechenah.*
A dullard gives full vent to rage, but a wise person calms it down.

**12**
מֹ֭שֵׁל מַקְשִׁ֣יב עַל־דְּבַר־שָׁ֑קֶר כׇּֽל־מְשָׁרְתָ֥יו רְשָׁעִֽים׃
*Moshel maqshiv al-devar-shaqer, kol-mesharetav resha'im.*
A ruler who listens to lies — all his ministers will be wicked.

**13**
רָ֤שׁ וְאִ֣ישׁ תְּכָכִ֣ים נִפְגָּ֑שׁוּ מֵ֤אִיר־עֵינֵ֖י שְׁנֵיהֶ֣ם יְהֹוָֽה׃
*Rash ve-ish tekhakhim nifgashu, me'ir-einei sheneihem Adonai.*
The poor and the fraudulent meet; GOD gives luster to the eyes of both.

**14**
מֶ֤לֶךְ שׁוֹפֵ֣ט בֶּאֱמֶ֣ת דַּלִּ֑ים כִּ֝סְא֗וֹ לָעַ֥ד יִכּֽוֹן׃
*Melekh shofet be-emet dallim, kis'o la-ad yikkon.*
A king who judges the wretched honestly — his throne will be established forever.

**15**
שֵׁ֣בֶט וְ֭תוֹכַחַת יִתֵּ֣ן חׇכְמָ֑ה וְנַ֥עַר מְ֝שֻׁלָּ֗ח מֵבִ֥ישׁ אִמּֽוֹ׃
*Shevet ve-tokhachat yitten chokhmah, ve-na'ar meshullach mevish immo.*
Rod and reproof produce wisdom, but a youth out of control is a disgrace to his mother.

**16**
בִּרְב֣וֹת רְ֭שָׁעִים יִרְבֶּה־פָּ֑שַׁע וְ֝צַדִּיקִ֗ים בְּֽמַפַּלְתָּ֥ם יִרְאֽוּ׃
*Bi-revot resha'im yirbeh-fasha, ve-tzaddiqim be-mappaltam yir'u.*
When the wicked increase, offenses increase, but the righteous will see their downfall.

**17**
יַסֵּ֣ר בִּ֭נְךָ וִינִיחֶ֑ךָ וְיִתֵּ֖ן מַעֲדַנִּ֣ים לְנַפְשֶֽׁךָ׃
*Yasser binkha vi-yniichekha, ve-yitten ma'adannim le-nafshekha.*
Discipline your son and he will give you peace; he will gratify you with dainties.

**18**
בְּאֵ֣ין חָ֭זוֹן יִפָּ֣רַֽע עָ֑ם וְשֹׁמֵ֖ר תּוֹרָ֣ה אַשְׁרֵֽהוּ׃
*Be-ein chazon yippara am, ve-shomer torah ashrehu.*
For lack of vision a people lose restraint, but happy is he who heeds instruction.

**19**
בִּ֭דְבָרִים לֹא־יִוָּ֣סֶר עָ֑בֶד כִּי־יָ֝בִ֗ין וְאֵ֣ין מַעֲנֶֽה׃
*Bi-dvarim lo-yivvasor aved, ki-yavin ve-ein ma'aneh.*
A slave cannot be disciplined by words; though he may comprehend, he does not respond.

**20**
חָזִ֗יתָ אִ֭ישׁ אָ֣ץ בִּדְבָרָ֑יו תִּקְוָ֖ה לִכְסִ֣יל מִמֶּֽנּוּ׃
*Chazita ish atz bi-dvarav, tiqvah li-khesil mimmennu.*
If you see a man hasty in speech, there is more hope for a fool than for him.

**21**
מְפַנֵּ֣ק מִנֹּ֣עַר עַבְדּ֑וֹ וְ֝אַחֲרִית֗וֹ יִהְיֶ֥ה מָנֽוֹן׃
*Mefanneq mi-no'ar avdo, ve-acharito yihyeh manon.*
A slave pampered from youth will come to a bad end.

**22**
אִֽישׁ־אַ֭ף יְגָרֶ֣ה מָד֑וֹן וּבַ֖עַל חֵמָ֣ה רַב־פָּֽשַׁע׃
*Ish-af yegareh madon, u-va'al chemah rav-pasha.*
An angry man provokes a quarrel; a hothead commits many offenses.

**23**
גַּאֲוַ֣ת אָ֭דָם תַּשְׁפִּילֶ֑נּוּ וּשְׁפַל־ר֝֗וּחַ יִתְמֹ֥ךְ כָּבֽוֹד׃
*Ga'avat adam tashppilennu, u-shefal-ruach yitmokh kavod.*
Pride of mortals will bring humiliation, but one who is humble will obtain honor.

**24**
חוֹלֵ֣ק עִם־גַּ֭נָּב שׂוֹנֵ֣א נַפְשׁ֑וֹ אָלָ֥ה יִ֝שְׁמַ֗ע וְלֹ֣א יַגִּֽיד׃
*Choleq im-gannav soneh nafsho, alah yishma ve-lo yaggid.*
He who shares with a thief is his own enemy; he hears the imprecation and does not tell.

**25**
חֶרְדַּ֣ת אָ֭דָם יִתֵּ֣ן מוֹקֵ֑שׁ וּבוֹטֵ֖חַ בַּיהֹוָ֣ה יְשֻׂגָּֽב׃
*Cherdat adam yitten moqesh, u-voteiach ba-Adonai yesuggav.*
A person's fears become a trap, but one who trusts in GOD shall be safeguarded.

**26**
רַ֭בִּים מְבַקְשִׁ֣ים פְּנֵֽי־מוֹשֵׁ֑ל וּ֝מֵיְהֹוָ֗ה מִשְׁפַּט־אִֽישׁ׃
*Rabbim mevaqshim penei-moshel, u-me-Adonai mishpat-ish.*
Many seek audience with a ruler, but it is from GOD that a wronged party gets justice.

**27**
תּוֹעֲבַ֣ת צַ֭דִּיקִים אִ֣ישׁ עָ֑וֶל וְתוֹעֲבַ֖ת רָשָׁ֣ע יְשַׁר־דָּֽרֶךְ׃
*To'avat tzaddiqim ish avel, ve-to'avat rasha yeshar-derekh.*
The unjust man is an abomination to the righteous, and one whose way is straight is an abomination to the wicked.
