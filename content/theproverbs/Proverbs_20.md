## Verses 1–30: Wisdom, Kings, and Human Nature

**1**
לֵ֣ץ הַ֭יַּיִן הֹמֶ֣ה שֵׁכָ֑ר וְכׇל־שֹׁ֥גֶה בּ֝֗וֹ לֹ֣א יֶחְכָּֽם׃
*Letz ha-yayin homeh shekhar, ve-khol-shogeh bo lo yechkam.*
Wine is a scoffer, strong drink a roisterer;
no one who is muddled by them will ever grow wise.

**2**
נַ֣הַם כַּ֭כְּפִיר אֵ֣ימַת מֶ֑לֶךְ מִ֝תְעַבְּר֗וֹ חוֹטֵ֥א נַפְשֽׁוֹ׃
*Naham ka-khefir eimat melekh, mit'abbero chote nafsho.*
The terror of a king is like the roar of a lion;
whoever provokes it forfeits life.

**3**
כָּב֣וֹד לָ֭אִישׁ שֶׁ֣בֶת מֵרִ֑יב וְכׇל־אֱ֝וִ֗יל יִתְגַּלָּֽע׃
*Khavod la-ish shevet me-riv, ve-khol-evil yitgalla.*
It is honorable for a man to desist from strife,
but every fool becomes embroiled.

**4**
מֵ֭חֹרֶף עָצֵ֣ל לֹא־יַחֲרֹ֑שׁ וְשָׁאַ֖ל בַּקָּצִ֣יר וָאָֽיִן׃
*Me-choref atzel lo-yacharosh, ve-sha'al ba-qatzir va-ayin.*
In winter sluggards do not plow;
at harvesttime they seek, and find nothing.

**5**
מַ֣יִם עֲ֭מֻקִּים עֵצָ֣ה בְלֶב־אִ֑ישׁ וְאִ֖ישׁ תְּבוּנָ֣ה יִדְלֶֽנָּה׃
*Mayim amuqqim etzah ve-lev-ish, ve-ish tevunah yidlennah.*
The designs in a person's mind are deep waters,
but a man of understanding can draw them out.

**6**
רׇב־אָדָ֗ם יִ֭קְרָא אִ֣ישׁ חַסְדּ֑וֹ וְאִ֥ישׁ אֱ֝מוּנִ֗ים מִ֣י יִמְצָֽא׃
*Rav-adam yiqra ish chasdo, ve-ish emunim mi yimtza.*
He calls many a man his loyal friend,
but who can find a faithful man?

**7**
מִתְהַלֵּ֣ךְ בְּתֻמּ֣וֹ צַדִּ֑יק אַשְׁרֵ֖י בָנָ֣יו אַחֲרָֽיו׃
*Mithalekh be-tummo tzaddiq, ashrei vanav acharav.*
The righteous live blamelessly;
happy are their children who come after them.

**8**
מֶ֗לֶךְ יוֹשֵׁ֥ב עַל־כִּסֵּא־דִ֑ין מְזָרֶ֖ה בְעֵינָ֣יו כׇּל־רָֽע׃
*Melekh yoshev al-kisse-din, mezareh ve-einav kol-ra.*
The king seated on the throne of judgment
can winnow out all evil by his glance.

**9**
מִֽי־יֹ֭אמַר זִכִּ֣יתִי לִבִּ֑י טָ֝הַ֗רְתִּי מֵחַטָּאתִֽי׃
*Mi-yomar zikkiti libbi, taharti me-chattati.*
Who can say, "I have cleansed my heart,
I am purged of my sin"?

**10**
אֶ֣בֶן וָ֭אֶבֶן אֵיפָ֣ה וְאֵיפָ֑ה תּוֹעֲבַ֥ת יְ֝הֹוָ֗ה גַּם־שְׁנֵיהֶֽם׃
*Even va-even eifah ve-eifah, to'avat Adonai gam-sheneihem.*
False weights and false measures —
both are an abomination to G.

**11**
גַּ֣ם בְּ֭מַעֲלָלָיו יִתְנַכֶּר־נָ֑עַר אִם־זַ֖ךְ וְאִם־יָשָׁ֣ר פׇּעֳלֽוֹ׃
*Gam be-ma'alalav yitnaqqer-na'ar, im-zakh ve-im-yashar po'olo.*
Children may be dissembling in their behavior
even though their actions are blameless and proper.

**12**
אֹ֣זֶן שֹׁ֭מַעַת וְעַ֣יִן רֹאָ֑ה יְ֝הֹוָ֗ה עָשָׂ֥ה גַם־שְׁנֵיהֶֽם׃
*Ozen shoma'at ve-ayin ro'ah, Adonai asah gam-sheneihem.*
The ear that hears, the eye that sees —
G made them both.

**13**
אַל־תֶּאֱהַ֣ב שֵׁ֭נָה פֶּן־תִּוָּרֵ֑שׁ פְּקַ֖ח עֵינֶ֣יךָ שְֽׂבַֽע־לָֽחֶם׃
*Al-te'ehav shenah pen-tivvaresh, peqach einekha seva-lachem.*
Do not love sleep lest you be impoverished;
keep your eyes open and you will have plenty of food.

**14**
רַ֣ע רַ֭ע יֹאמַ֣ר הַקּוֹנֶ֑ה וְאֹזֵ֥ל ל֝֗וֹ אָ֣ז יִתְהַלָּֽל׃
*Ra ra yomar ha-qoneh, ve-ozel lo az yithallal.*
"Bad, bad," says the buyer,
then goes away in self-congratulation.

**15**
יֵ֣שׁ זָ֭הָב וְרׇב־פְּנִינִ֑ים וּכְלִ֥י יְ֝קָ֗ר שִׂפְתֵי־דָֽעַת׃
*Yesh zahav ve-rav-peninim, u-kheli yeqar siftei-da'at.*
Gold is plentiful, jewels abundant,
but wise speech is a precious object.

**16**
לְֽקַח־בִּ֭גְדוֹ כִּי־עָ֣רַב זָ֑ר וּבְעַ֖ד נׇכְרִיָּ֣ה חַבְלֵֽהוּ׃
*Leqach-bigdo ki-arav zar, u-ve'ad nokhriyyah chavlehu.*
Seize his garment, for he stood surety for another;
take it as a pledge, for he stood surety for an unfamiliar woman.

**17**
עָרֵ֣ב לָ֭אִישׁ לֶ֣חֶם שָׁ֑קֶר וְ֝אַחַ֗ר יִמָּֽלֵא־פִ֥יהוּ חָצָֽץ׃
*Arev la-ish lechem shaqer, ve-achar yimmale-fihu chatzatz.*
Bread gained by fraud may be tasty to a man,
but later his mouth will be filled with gravel.

**18**
מַ֭חֲשָׁבוֹת בְּעֵצָ֣ה תִכּ֑וֹן וּ֝בְתַחְבֻּל֗וֹת עֲשֵׂ֣ה מִלְחָמָֽה׃
*Machashavot be-etzah tikkon, u-ve-tachbulot aseh milchamah.*
Plans laid in council will succeed;
wage war with stratagems.

**19**
גּֽוֹלֶה־סּ֭וֹד הוֹלֵ֣ךְ רָכִ֑יל וּלְפֹתֶ֥ה שְׂ֝פָתָ֗יו לֹ֣א תִתְעָרָֽב׃
*Goleh-sod holekh rakhil, u-le-foteh sefatav lo tit'arav.*
Whoever gives away secrets is base;
do not take up with a big-mouth.

**20**
מְ֭קַלֵּל אָבִ֣יו וְאִמּ֑וֹ יִֽדְעַ֥ךְ נֵ֝ר֗וֹ בֶּאֱשׁ֥וּן חֹֽשֶׁךְ׃
*Meqallel aviv ve-immo, yid'akh nero be-eshun choshekh.*
Whoever reviles their father or mother
will be without lamplight when darkness comes.

**21**
נַ֭חֲלָה מְבֹהֶ֣לֶת בָּרִאשׁוֹנָ֑ה וְ֝אַחֲרִיתָ֗הּ לֹ֣א תְבֹרָֽךְ׃
*Nachalah mevohellet ba-rishonah, ve-acharitah lo tevorakh.*
An estate acquired in haste at the outset
will not be blessed in the end.

**22**
אַל־תֹּאמַ֥ר אֲשַׁלְּמָה־רָ֑ע קַוֵּ֥ה לַ֝יהֹוָ֗ה וְיֹ֣שַֽׁע לָֽךְ׃
*Al-tomar ashallemah-ra, qavveh la-Adonai ve-yosha lakh.*
Do not say, "I will requite evil";
put your hope in G and you will be delivered.

**23**
תּוֹעֲבַ֣ת יְ֭הֹוָה אֶ֣בֶן וָאָ֑בֶן וּמֹאזְנֵ֖י מִרְמָ֣ה לֹא־טֽוֹב׃
*To'avat Adonai even va-aven, u-mo'znei mirmah lo-tov.*
False weights are an abomination to G;
dishonest scales are not right.

**24**
מֵיְהֹוָ֥ה מִצְעֲדֵי־גָ֑בֶר וְ֝אָדָ֗ם מַה־יָּבִ֥ין דַּרְכּֽוֹ׃
*Me-Adonai mitz'adei-gaver, ve-adam mah-yavin darko.*
People's steps are decided by G;
what do mortals know about their own way?

**25**
מוֹקֵ֣שׁ אָ֭דָם יָ֣לַע קֹ֑דֶשׁ וְאַחַ֖ר נְדָרִ֣ים לְבַקֵּֽר׃
*Moqesh adam yala qodesh, ve-achar nedarim levaqer.*
It is a snare for mortals to pledge a sacred gift rashly
and after the vows to reconsider.

**26**
מְזָרֶ֣ה רְ֭שָׁעִים מֶ֣לֶךְ חָכָ֑ם וַיָּ֖שֶׁב עֲלֵיהֶ֣ם אוֹפָֽן׃
*Mezareh resha'im melekh chakham, va-yashev aleihem ofan.*
A wise king winnows out the wicked,
and turns the wheel upon them.

**27**
נֵ֣ר יְ֭הֹוָה נִשְׁמַ֣ת אָדָ֑ם חֹ֝פֵ֗שׂ כׇּל־חַדְרֵי־בָֽטֶן׃
*Ner Adonai nishmat adam, chofes kol-chadreit-vaten.*
A mortal's lifebreath is G's lamp
revealing all their inmost parts.

**28**
חֶ֣סֶד וֶ֭אֱמֶת יִצְּרוּ־מֶ֑לֶךְ וְסָעַ֖ד בַּחֶ֣סֶד כִּסְאֽוֹ׃
*Chesed ve-emet yitzeru-melekh, ve-sa'ad ba-chesed kis'o.*
Faithfulness and loyalty protect the king;
he maintains his throne by faithfulness.

**29**
תִּפְאֶ֣רֶת בַּחוּרִ֣ים כֹּחָ֑ם וַהֲדַ֖ר זְקֵנִ֣ים שֵׂיבָֽה׃
*Tif'eret bachurim kocham, va-hadar zeqenim seivah.*
The glory of young men is their strength;
the majesty of elders is their gray hair.

**30**
חַבֻּר֣וֹת פֶּ֭צַע תַּמְר֣וּק בְּרָ֑ע וּ֝מַכּ֗וֹת חַדְרֵי־בָֽטֶן׃
*Chavurot petza tamruq be-ra, u-makkot chadreit-vaten.*
Bruises and wounds are repayment for evil,
striking at one's inmost parts.
