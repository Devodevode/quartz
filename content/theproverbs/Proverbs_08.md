## Verses 1–11: Wisdom's Call

**1**
הֲלֹֽא־חׇכְמָ֥ה תִקְרָ֑א וּ֝תְבוּנָ֗ה תִּתֵּ֥ן קוֹלָֽהּ׃
*Halo-chokhmah tiqra, u-tevunah titten qolah.*
It is Wisdom calling, Understanding raising her voice.

**2**
בְּרֹאשׁ־מְרֹמִ֥ים עֲלֵי־דָ֑רֶךְ בֵּ֖ית נְתִיב֣וֹת נִצָּֽבָה׃
*Be-rosh meromim alei-darekh, beit netivot nitzavah.*
She takes her stand at the topmost heights, by the wayside, at the crossroads.

**3**
לְיַד־שְׁעָרִ֥ים לְפִי־קָ֑רֶת מְב֖וֹא פְתָחִ֣ים תָּרֹֽנָּה׃
*Le-yad she'arim le-fi-qaret, mevo fetachim taronah.*
Near the gates at the city entrance; at the entryways, she shouts.

**4**
אֲלֵיכֶ֣ם אִישִׁ֣ים אֶקְרָ֑א וְ֝קוֹלִ֗י אֶל־בְּנֵ֥י אָדָֽם׃
*Aleikhem ishim eqra, ve-qoli el-benei adam.*
"O human race, I call to you; my cry is to all mortals.

**5**
הָבִ֣ינוּ פְתָאיִ֣ם עׇרְמָ֑ה וּ֝כְסִילִ֗ים הָבִ֥ינוּ לֵֽב׃
*Havinu feta'yim ormah, u-khesilim havinu lev.*
O simple ones, learn shrewdness; O dullards, instruct your minds.

**6**
שִׁ֭מְעוּ כִּֽי־נְגִידִ֣ים אֲדַבֵּ֑ר וּמִפְתַּ֥ח שְׂ֝פָתַ֗י מֵישָׁרִֽים׃
*Shim'u ki-negidim adabber, u-miftach sefatai mesharim.*
Listen, for I speak noble things; uprightness comes from my lips.

**7**
כִּֽי־אֱ֭מֶת יֶהְגֶּ֣ה חִכִּ֑י וְתוֹעֲבַ֖ת שְׂפָתַ֣י רֶֽשַׁע׃
*Ki-emet yehgeh chikki, ve-to'evat sefatai resha.*
My mouth utters truth; wickedness is abhorrent to my lips.

**8**
בְּצֶ֥דֶק כׇּל־אִמְרֵי־פִ֑י אֵ֥ין בָּ֝הֶ֗ם נִפְתָּ֥ל וְעִקֵּֽשׁ׃
*Be-tzedeq kol-imrei-fi, ein bahem niftal ve-iqqesh.*
All my words are just, none of them perverse or crooked.

**9**
כֻּלָּ֣ם נְ֭כֹחִים לַמֵּבִ֑ין וִ֝ישָׁרִ֗ים לְמֹ֣צְאֵי דָֽעַת׃
*Kullam nekochim la-mevin, vi-yesharim le-motze'ei da'at.*
All are straightforward to anyone with intelligence, and right to those who have attained knowledge.

**10**
קְחֽוּ־מוּסָרִ֥י וְאַל־כָּ֑סֶף וְ֝דַ֗עַת מֵחָר֥וּץ נִבְחָֽר׃
*Qechu-musari ve-al-kasef, ve-da'at me-charutz nivchar.*
Accept my discipline rather than silver, knowledge rather than choice gold.

**11**
כִּֽי־טוֹבָ֣ה חׇ֭כְמָה מִפְּנִינִ֑ים וְכׇל־חֲ֝פָצִ֗ים לֹ֣א יִֽשְׁווּ־בָֽהּ׃
*Ki-tovah chokhmah mi-peninim, ve-khol-chafatzim lo yishvu-vah.*
For wisdom is better than rubies; no goods can equal her.

## Verses 12–21: Wisdom's Nature and Gifts

**12**
אֲֽנִי־חׇ֭כְמָה שָׁכַ֣נְתִּי עׇרְמָ֑ה וְדַ֖עַת מְזִמּ֣וֹת אֶמְצָֽא׃
*Ani-chokhmah shakhanti ormah, ve-da'at mezimmot emtza.*
"I, Wisdom, live with Prudence; I attain knowledge and foresight.

**13**
יִ֥רְאַ֣ת יְהֹוָה֮ שְֽׂנֹ֫את־רָ֥ע גֵּ֘אָ֤ה וְגָא֨וֹן ׀ וְדֶ֣רֶךְ רָ֭ע וּפִ֨י תַהְפֻּכ֬וֹת שָׂנֵֽאתִי׃
*Yir'at Adonai seno'at-ra, ge'ah ve-ga'on ve-derekh ra u-fi tahpukhot saneti.*
To fear GOD is to hate evil; I hate pride, arrogance, the evil way, and duplicity in speech.

**14**
לִֽי־עֵ֭צָה וְתוּשִׁיָּ֑ה אֲנִ֥י בִ֝ינָ֗ה לִ֣י גְבוּרָֽה׃
*Li-etzah ve-tushiyyah, ani vinah, li gevurah.*
Mine are counsel and resourcefulness; I am understanding; courage is mine.

**15**
בִּ֭י מְלָכִ֣ים יִמְלֹ֑כוּ וְ֝רֹזְנִ֗ים יְחֹ֣קְקוּ צֶֽדֶק׃
*Bi melakhim yimlokhu, ve-rozenim yechoqequ tzedeq.*
Through me kings reign and rulers decree just laws.

**16**
בִּ֭י שָׂרִ֣ים יָשֹׂ֑רוּ וּ֝נְדִיבִ֗ים כׇּל־שֹׁ֥פְטֵי צֶֽדֶק׃
*Bi sarim yasoru, u-nedivim kol-shoftei tzedeq.*
Through me princes rule, great men and all the righteous judges.

**17**
אֲ֭נִי אֹהֲבַ֣י אֵהָ֑ב וּ֝מְשַׁחֲרַ֗י יִמְצָאֻֽנְנִי׃
*Ani ohavai ehav, u-meshacharai yimtza'unni.*
Those who love me I love, and those who seek me will find me.

**18**
עֹֽשֶׁר־וְכָב֥וֹד אִתִּ֑י ה֥וֹן עָ֝תֵ֗ק וּצְדָקָֽה׃
*Osher-ve-khavod itti, hon ateq u-tzedaqah.*
Riches and honor belong to me, enduring wealth and success.

**19**
ט֣וֹב פִּ֭רְיִי מֵחָר֣וּץ וּמִפָּ֑ז וּ֝תְבוּאָתִ֗י מִכֶּ֥סֶף נִבְחָֽר׃
*Tov piryi me-charutz u-mi-paz, u-tevuati mi-kesef nivchar.*
My fruit is better than gold, fine gold, and my produce better than choice silver.

**20**
בְּאֹֽרַח־צְדָקָ֥ה אֲהַלֵּ֑ךְ בְּ֝ת֗וֹךְ נְתִיב֥וֹת מִשְׁפָּֽט׃
*Be-orach-tzedaqah ahalekh, be-tokh netivot mishpat.*
I walk on the way of righteousness, on the paths of justice.

**21**
לְהַנְחִ֖יל אֹהֲבַ֥י ׀ יֵ֑שׁ וְאֹצְרֹ֖תֵיהֶ֣ם אֲמַלֵּֽא׃
*Le-hanchil ohavai yesh, ve-otzroteihem amalei.*
I endow those who love me with substance; I will fill their treasuries.

## Verses 22–31: Wisdom's Eternal Origin

**22**
יְֽהֹוָ֗ה קָ֭נָנִי רֵאשִׁ֣ית דַּרְכּ֑וֹ קֶ֖דֶם מִפְעָלָ֣יו מֵאָֽז׃
*Adonai qanani reshit darko, qedem mif'alav me'az.*
"I was created at the beginning of GOD's course, as the first of the works of old.

**23**
מֵ֭עוֹלָם נִסַּ֥כְתִּי מֵרֹ֗אשׁ מִקַּדְמֵי־אָֽרֶץ׃
*Me'olam nissakti me-rosh, mi-qadmei-aretz.*
In the distant past I was fashioned, at the beginning, at the origin of earth.

**24**
בְּאֵין־תְּהֹמ֥וֹת חוֹלָ֑לְתִּי בְּאֵ֥ין מַ֝עְיָנ֗וֹת נִכְבַּדֵּי־מָֽיִם׃
*Be-ein-tehomot cholalti, be-ein ma'yanot nikhbaddei-mayim.*
There was still no deep when I was brought forth, no springs rich in water.

**25**
בְּטֶ֣רֶם הָרִ֣ים הׇטְבָּ֑עוּ לִפְנֵ֖י גְבָע֣וֹת חוֹלָֽלְתִּי׃
*Be-terem harim hotba'u, lifnei geva'ot cholalti.*
Before the foundations of the mountains were sunk, before the hills I was born.

**26**
עַד־לֹ֣א עָ֭שָׂה אֶ֣רֶץ וְחוּצ֑וֹת וְ֝רֹ֗אשׁ עַפְר֥וֹת תֵּבֵֽל׃
*Ad-lo asah eretz ve-chutzot, ve-rosh afrot tevel.*
Earth and fields had not yet been made, or the world's first clumps of clay.

**27**
בַּהֲכִינ֣וֹ שָׁ֭מַיִם שָׁ֣ם אָ֑נִי בְּחֻ֥קוֹ ח֝֗וּג עַל־פְּנֵ֥י תְהֽוֹם׃
*Ba-hakino shamayim sham ani, be-chuqo chug al-penei tehom.*
I was there when the heavens were set into place; when the horizon was fixed upon the deep.

**28**
בְּאַמְּצ֣וֹ שְׁחָקִ֣ים מִמָּ֑עַל בַּ֝עֲז֗וֹז עִינ֥וֹת תְּהֽוֹם׃
*Be-amtzo shehaqim mi-ma'al, ba-azoz einot tehom.*
When the heavens above were made firm, and the fountains of the deep gushed forth.

**29**
בְּשׂ֘וּמ֤וֹ לַיָּ֨ם ׀ חֻקּ֗וֹ וּ֭מַיִם לֹ֣א יַֽעַבְרוּ־פִ֑יו בְּ֝חוּק֗וֹ מ֣וֹסְדֵי אָֽרֶץ׃
*Be-sumo la-yam chuqqo u-mayim lo ya'avru-fiv, be-chuqo mosdei aretz.*
When the sea was assigned its limits, so that its waters never transgress GOD's command; when the foundations of the earth were fixed.

**30**
וָאֶהְיֶ֥ה אֶצְל֗וֹ אָ֫מ֥וֹן וָאֶהְיֶ֣ה שַׁ֭עֲשׁוּעִים י֤וֹם ׀ י֑וֹם מְשַׂחֶ֖קֶת לְפָנָ֣יו בְּכׇל־עֵֽת׃
*Va-ehyeh etzlo amon, va-ehyeh sha'ashu'im yom yom, mesacheqet lefanav be-khol-et.*
I was with God as a confidant, a source of delight every day, rejoicing before God at all times.

**31**
מְ֭שַׂחֶקֶת בְּתֵבֵ֣ל אַרְצ֑וֹ וְ֝שַׁעֲשֻׁעַ֗י אֶת־בְּנֵ֥י אָדָֽם׃
*Mesacheqet be-tevel artzo, ve-sha'ashu'ai et-benei adam.*
Rejoicing in GOD's inhabited world, finding delight with mortals.

## Verses 32–36: Wisdom's Final Appeal

**32**
וְעַתָּ֣ה בָ֭נִים שִׁמְעוּ־לִ֑י וְ֝אַשְׁרֵ֗י דְּרָכַ֥י יִשְׁמֹֽרוּ׃
*Ve-attah vanim shim'u-li, ve-ashrei derakhay yishmoru.*
Now, children, listen to me; happy are they who keep my ways.

**33**
שִׁמְע֖וּ מוּסָ֥ר וַחֲכָ֗מוּ וְאַל־תִּפְרָֽעוּ׃
*Shim'u musar va-chakamu, ve-al-tifra'u.*
Heed discipline and become wise; do not spurn it.

**34**
אַ֥שְֽׁרֵי־אָדָם֮ שֹׁמֵ֢עַֽ֫ לִ֥י לִשְׁקֹ֣ד עַל־דַּ֭לְתֹתַי י֤וֹם ׀ י֑וֹם לִ֝שְׁמֹ֗ר מְזוּזֹ֥ת פְּתָחָֽי׃
*Ashrei-adam shome'a li, lishqod al-daltetai yom yom, lishmor mezuzot petachai.*
Happy is the one who listens to me, coming early to my gates each day, waiting outside my doors.

**35**
כִּ֣י מֹ֭צְאִי מָצָ֣א חַיִּ֑ים וַיָּ֥פֶק רָ֝צ֗וֹן מֵיְהֹוָֽה׃
*Ki motze'i matza chayyim, va-yafek ratzon me-Adonai.*
For one who finds me finds life and obtains favor from GOD.

**36**
וְֽ֭חֹטְאִי חֹמֵ֣ס נַפְשׁ֑וֹ כׇּל־מְ֝שַׂנְאַ֗י אָ֣הֲבוּ מָֽוֶת׃
*Ve-chotei chomes nafsho, kol-mesan'ai ahavu mavet.*
But one who misses me self-destructs; all who hate me love death."
