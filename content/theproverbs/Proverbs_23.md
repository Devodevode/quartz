## Verses 1–35: Counsel, Warning, and the Dangers of Wine

**1**
כִּֽי־תֵ֭שֵׁב לִלְח֣וֹם אֶת־מוֹשֵׁ֑ל בִּ֥ין תָּ֝בִ֗ין אֶת־אֲשֶׁ֥ר לְפָנֶֽיךָ׃
*Ki-teshev li-lchom et-moshel, bin tavin et-asher lefanekha.*
When you sit down to dine with a ruler,
consider well who is before you.

**2**
וְשַׂמְתָּ֣ שַׂכִּ֣ין בְּלֹעֶ֑ךָ אִם־בַּ֖עַל נֶ֣פֶשׁ אָֽתָּה׃
*Ve-samta sakkin be-lo'ekha, im-ba'al nefesh attah.*
Thrust a knife into your gullet
if you have a large appetite.

**3**
אַל־תִּ֭תְאָו לְמַטְעַמּוֹתָ֑יו וְ֝ה֗וּא לֶ֣חֶם כְּזָבִֽים׃
*Al-tit'av le-mat'ammotav, ve-hu lechem kezavim.*
Do not crave the dainties,
for they are counterfeit food.

**4**
אַל־תִּיגַ֥ע לְֽהַעֲשִׁ֑יר מִֽבִּינָתְךָ֥ חֲדָֽל׃
*Al-tiga le-ha'ashir, mi-binatekha chadal.*
Do not toil to gain wealth;
have the sense to desist.

**5**
הֲתָ֤עִיף־עֵינֶ֥יךָ בּ֗וֹ וְֽאֵ֫ינֶ֥נּוּ כִּ֤י עָשֹׂ֣ה יַעֲשֶׂה־לּ֣וֹ כְנָפַ֑יִם כְּ֝נֶ֗שֶׁר יָע֥וּף הַשָּׁמָֽיִם׃
*Ha-ta'if-einekha vo ve-einennu, ki asoh ya'aseh-lo khenafayim, ke-nesher ya'uf ha-shamayim.*
You see it, then it is gone;
it grows wings and flies away,
like an eagle, heavenward.

**6**
אַל־תִּלְחַ֗ם אֶת־לֶ֭חֶם רַ֣ע עָ֑יִן וְאַל־תִּ֝תְאָ֗ו לְמַטְעַמֹּתָֽיו׃
*Al-tilcham et-lechem ra ayin, ve-al-tit'av le-mat'ammotav.*
Do not eat of a miser's food;
do not crave for his dainties;

**7**
כִּ֤י כְּמ֥וֹ שָׁעַ֥ר בְּנַפְשׁ֗וֹ כֶּ֫ן־ה֥וּא אֱכ֣וֹל וּ֭שְׁתֵה יֹ֣אמַר לָ֑ךְ וְ֝לִבּ֗וֹ בַּל־עִמָּֽךְ׃
*Ki ke-mo sha'ar be-nafsho ken-hu, ekhol u-shtei yomar lakh, ve-libbo bal-immakh.*
he is like one keeping accounts;
"Eat and drink," he says to you,
but he does not really mean it.

**8**
פִּֽתְּךָ־אָכַ֥לְתָּ תְקִיאֶ֑נָּה וְ֝שִׁחַ֗תָּ דְּבָרֶ֥יךָ הַנְּעִימִֽים׃
*Pitkha-akhalta teqi'ennah, ve-shichatta devarekha ha-ne'imim.*
The morsel you eat you will vomit;
you will waste your courteous words.

**9**
בְּאׇזְנֵ֣י כְ֭סִיל אַל־תְּדַבֵּ֑ר כִּי־יָ֝ב֗וּז לְשֵׂ֣כֶל מִלֶּֽיךָ׃
*Be-oznei khesil al-tedabber, ki-yavuz le-sekhel millekha.*
Do not speak to a dullard —
who will disdain your sensible words.

**10**
אַל־תַּ֭סֵּג גְּב֣וּל עוֹלָ֑ם וּבִשְׂדֵ֥י יְ֝תוֹמִ֗ים אַל־תָּבֹֽא׃
*Al-tasseg gevul olam, u-vi-sdei yetomim al-tavo.*
Do not remove ancient boundary stones;
do not encroach upon the field of orphans,

**11**
כִּֽי־גֹאֲלָ֥ם חָזָ֑ק הֽוּא־יָרִ֖יב אֶת־רִיבָ֣ם אִתָּֽךְ׃
*Ki-go'alam chazaq, hu-yariv et-rivam ittakh.*
for they have a mighty Redeemer,
who will surely take up their cause with you.

**12**
הָבִ֣יאָה לַמּוּסָ֣ר לִבֶּ֑ךָ וְ֝אׇזְנֶ֗ךָ לְאִמְרֵי־דָֽעַת׃
*Havi'ah la-musar libbkha, ve-oznekha le-imrei-da'at.*
Apply your mind to discipline
and your ears to wise sayings.

**13**
אַל־תִּמְנַ֣ע מִנַּ֣עַר מוּסָ֑ר כִּֽי־תַכֶּ֥נּוּ בַ֝שֵּׁ֗בֶט לֹ֣א יָמֽוּת׃
*Al-timna mi-na'ar musar, ki-takhkennu va-shevet lo yamut.*
Do not withhold discipline from children;
if you beat them with a rod they will not die.

**14**
אַ֭תָּה בַּשֵּׁ֣בֶט תַּכֶּ֑נּוּ וְ֝נַפְשׁ֗וֹ מִשְּׁא֥וֹל תַּצִּֽיל׃
*Attah ba-shevet takkennu, ve-nafsho mi-she'ol tatzil.*
Beat them with a rod
and you will save them from the grave.

**15**
בְּ֭נִי אִם־חָכַ֣ם לִבֶּ֑ךָ יִשְׂמַ֖ח לִבִּ֣י גַם־אָֽנִי׃
*Beni im-chakham libbkha, yismach libbi gam-ani.*
My son, if your mind gets wisdom,
my mind, too, will be gladdened.

**16**
וְתַעְלֹ֥זְנָה כִלְיוֹתָ֑י בְּדַבֵּ֥ר שְׂ֝פָתֶ֗יךָ מֵישָׁרִֽים׃
*Ve-ta'aloznah khilyotai, be-dabber sefatekha mesharim.*
I shall rejoice with all my heart
when your lips speak right things.

**17**
אַל־יְקַנֵּ֣א לִ֭בְּךָ בַּחַטָּאִ֑ים כִּ֥י אִם־בְּיִרְאַת־יְ֝הֹוָ֗ה כׇּל־הַיּֽוֹם׃
*Al-yeqannei libbekha ba-chattaim, ki im-be-yir'at-Adonai kol-ha-yom.*
Do not envy sinners in your heart,
but only those who fear G, at all times,

**18**
כִּ֭י אִם־יֵ֣שׁ אַחֲרִ֑ית וְ֝תִקְוָתְךָ֗ לֹ֣א תִכָּרֵֽת׃
*Ki im-yesh acharit, ve-tiqvatekha lo tikkaret.*
for then you will have a future,
and your hope will never fail.

**19**
שְׁמַע־אַתָּ֣ה בְנִ֣י וַחֲכָ֑ם וְאַשֵּׁ֖ר בַּדֶּ֣רֶךְ לִבֶּֽךָ׃
*Shema-attah veni va-chakham, ve-asher ba-derekh libbkha.*
Listen, my son, and get wisdom;
lead your mind in a proper path.

**20**
אַל־תְּהִ֥י בְסֹֽבְאֵי־יָ֑יִן בְּזֹלְלֵ֖י בָשָׂ֣ר לָֽמוֹ׃
*Al-tehi ve-sov'ei-yayin, be-zollei vasar lamo.*
Do not be of those who guzzle wine,
or glut themselves on meat,

**21**
כִּֽי־סֹבֵ֣א וְ֭זוֹלֵל יִוָּרֵ֑שׁ וּ֝קְרָעִ֗ים תַּלְבִּ֥ישׁ נוּמָֽה׃
*Ki-sove ve-zolel yivvaresh, u-qera'im talbish numah.*
for guzzlers and gluttons will be impoverished,
and drowsing will clothe you in tatters.

**22**
שְׁמַ֣ע לְ֭אָבִיךָ זֶ֣ה יְלָדֶ֑ךָ וְאַל־תָּ֝ב֗וּז כִּֽי־זָקְנָ֥ה אִמֶּֽךָ׃
*Shema le-avikha zeh yeladekha, ve-al-tavuz ki-zaqenah immekha.*
Listen to your father who begot you;
do not disdain your mother when she is old.

**23**
אֱמֶ֣ת קְ֭נֵה וְאַל־תִּמְכֹּ֑ר חׇכְמָ֖ה וּמוּסָ֣ר וּבִינָֽה׃
*Emet qeneh ve-al-timkor, chokhmah u-musar u-vinah.*
Buy truth and never sell it,
and wisdom, discipline, and understanding.

**24**
גִּ֣יל יָ֭גִיל אֲבִ֣י צַדִּ֑יק וְיוֹלֵ֥ד חָ֝כָ֗ם יִשְׂמַח־בּֽוֹ׃
*Gil yagil avi tzaddiq, ve-yoled chakham yismach-bo.*
The father of a righteous person will exult;
he who begets a wise child will rejoice in them.

**25**
יִֽשְׂמַח־אָבִ֥יךָ וְאִמֶּ֑ךָ וְ֝תָגֵ֗ל יוֹלַדְתֶּֽךָ׃
*Yismach-avikha ve-immekha, ve-tagel yoladtekha.*
Your father and mother will rejoice;
she who bore you will exult.

**26**
תְּנָה־בְנִ֣י לִבְּךָ֣ לִ֑י וְ֝עֵינֶ֗יךָ דְּרָכַ֥י תִּצֹּֽרְנָה׃
*Tenah-veni libbkha li, ve-einekha derakhay titzzorenah.*
Give your mind to me, my son;
let your eyes watch my ways.

**27**
כִּֽי־שׁוּחָ֣ה עֲמֻקָּ֣ה זוֹנָ֑ה וּבְאֵ֥ר צָ֝רָ֗ה נׇכְרִיָּֽה׃
*Ki-shuchah amuqqah zonah, u-ve'er tzarah nokhriyyah.*
A prostitute is a deep pit;
a forbidden woman is a narrow well.

**28**
אַף־הִ֭יא כְּחֶ֣תֶף תֶּאֱרֹ֑ב וּ֝בוֹגְדִ֗ים בְּאָדָ֥ם תּוֹסִֽף׃
*Af-hi ke-chetef te'erov, u-vogdim be-adam tosif.*
She too lies in wait as if for prey,
and destroys the unfaithful among men.

**29**
לְמִ֨י א֥וֹי לְמִ֪י אֲב֡וֹי לְמִ֤י מִדְיָנִ֨ים לְמִ֥י שִׂ֗יחַ לְ֭מִי פְּצָעִ֣ים חִנָּ֑ם לְ֝מִ֗י חַכְלִל֥וּת עֵינָֽיִם׃
*Le-mi oy le-mi avoy, le-mi midyanim le-mi si'ach, le-mi petza'im chinnam, le-mi chakhlilut einayim.*
Who cries, "Woe!" who, "Alas!";
who has quarrels, who complaints;
who has wounds without cause;
who has bleary eyes?

**30**
לַֽמְאַחֲרִ֥ים עַל־הַיָּ֑יִן לַ֝בָּאִ֗ים לַחְקֹ֥ר מִמְסָֽךְ׃
*La-me'acharim al-ha-yayin, la-ba'im lachqor mimsakj.*
Those whom wine keeps till the small hours,
those who gather to drain the cups.

**31**
אַל־תֵּ֥רֶא יַּיִן֮ כִּ֤י יִתְאַ֫דָּ֥ם כִּֽי־יִתֵּ֣ן בַּכּ֣וֹס עֵינ֑וֹ יִ֝תְהַלֵּ֗ךְ בְּמֵֽישָׁרִֽים׃
*Al-tere yayin ki yit'adam, ki-yitten ba-kos eino, yithallekh be-mesharim.*
Do not ogle that red wine
as it lends its color to the cup,
as it flows on smoothly;

**32**
אַ֭חֲרִיתוֹ כְּנָחָ֣שׁ יִשָּׁ֑ךְ וּֽכְצִפְעֹנִ֥י יַפְרִֽשׁ׃
*Acharito ke-nachash yishshakh, u-khe-tzif'oni yafrish.*
in the end, it bites like a snake;
it spits like a basilisk.

**33**
עֵ֭ינֶיךָ יִרְא֣וּ זָר֑וֹת וְ֝לִבְּךָ֗ יְדַבֵּ֥ר תַּהְפֻּכֽוֹת׃
*Einekha yir'u zarot, ve-libbkha yedabber tahpukhot.*
Your eyes will see strange sights;
your heart will speak distorted things.

**34**
וְ֭הָיִיתָ כְּשֹׁכֵ֣ב בְּלֶב־יָ֑ם וּ֝כְשֹׁכֵ֗ב בְּרֹ֣אשׁ חִבֵּֽל׃
*Ve-hayita ke-shokhev be-lev-yam, u-khe-shokhev be-rosh chibbel.*
You will be like one lying in bed on high seas,
like one lying on top of the rigging.

**35**
הִכּ֥וּנִי בַל־חָלִיתִי֮ הֲלָמ֗וּנִי בַּל־יָ֫דָ֥עְתִּי מָתַ֥י אָקִ֑יץ א֝וֹסִ֗יף אֲבַקְשֶׁ֥נּוּ עֽוֹד׃
*Hikkuni val-chaliti, halamuni bal-yada'ti, matai aqutz osif avaqshennnu od.*
"They struck me, but I felt no hurt;
they beat me, but I was unaware;
as often as I wake,
I go after it again."
