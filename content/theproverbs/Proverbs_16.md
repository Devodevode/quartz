## Verses 1–33: GOD's Sovereignty and Human Conduct

**1**
לְאָדָ֥ם מַעַרְכֵי־לֵ֑ב וּ֝מֵיְהֹוָ֗ה מַעֲנֵ֥ה לָשֽׁוֹן׃
*Le-adam ma'arkhei-lev, u-me-Adonai ma'aneh lashon.*
Mortals may arrange their thoughts, but what they say depends on GOD.

**2**
כׇּֽל־דַּרְכֵי־אִ֭ישׁ זַ֣ךְ בְּעֵינָ֑יו וְתֹכֵ֖ן רוּח֣וֹת יְהֹוָֽה׃
*Kol-darkhei-ish zakh be-einav, ve-tokhen ruchot Adonai.*
People look approvingly on their own ways, but GOD probes motives.

**3**
גֹּ֣ל אֶל־יְהֹוָ֣ה מַעֲשֶׂ֑יךָ וְ֝יִכֹּ֗נוּ מַחְשְׁבֹתֶֽיךָ׃
*Gol el-Adonai ma'asekha, ve-yikonu machshevotekha.*
Entrust your affairs to GOD, and your plans will succeed.

**4**
כֹּ֤ל פָּעַ֣ל יְ֭הֹוָה לַֽמַּעֲנֵ֑הוּ וְגַם־רָ֝שָׁ֗ע לְי֣וֹם רָעָֽה׃
*Kol pa'al Adonai la-ma'anehu, ve-gam-rasha le-yom ra'ah.*
GOD made everything for a purpose, even the wicked for an evil day.

**5**
תּוֹעֲבַ֣ת יְ֭הֹוָה כׇּל־גְּבַהּ־לֵ֑ב יָ֥ד לְ֝יָ֗ד לֹ֣א יִנָּקֶֽה׃
*To'avat Adonai kol-gevah-lev, yad le-yad lo yinnaqeh.*
Everyone haughty is an abomination to GOD; assuredly, they will not go unpunished.

**6**
בְּחֶ֣סֶד וֶ֭אֱמֶת יְכֻפַּ֣ר עָוֺ֑ן וּבְיִרְאַ֥ת יְ֝הֹוָ֗ה ס֣וּר מֵרָֽע׃
*Be-chesed ve-emet yekuppar avon, u-ve-yir'at Adonai sur me-ra.*
Iniquity is expiated by loyalty and faithfulness, and evil is avoided through fear of GOD.

**7**
בִּרְצ֣וֹת יְ֭הֹוָה דַּרְכֵי־אִ֑ישׁ גַּם־א֝וֹיְבָ֗יו יַשְׁלִ֥ם אִתּֽוֹ׃
*Bi-retzot Adonai darkhei-ish, gam-oyevav yashlim itto.*
GOD, when pleased with someone's conduct, may turn even their enemies into allies.

**8**
טוֹב־מְ֭עַט בִּצְדָקָ֑ה מֵרֹ֥ב תְּ֝בוּא֗וֹת בְּלֹ֣א מִשְׁפָּֽט׃
*Tov-me'at bi-tzedaqah, me-rov tevuot be-lo mishpat.*
Better a little with righteousness than a large income with injustice.

**9**
לֵ֣ב אָ֭דָם יְחַשֵּׁ֣ב דַּרְכּ֑וֹ וַ֝יהֹוָ֗ה יָכִ֥ין צַעֲדֽוֹ׃
*Lev adam yechashev darko, va-Adonai yakhin tza'ado.*
Mortals may plot out their course, but it is GOD who directs their steps.

**10**
קֶ֤סֶם עַֽל־שִׂפְתֵי־מֶ֑לֶךְ בְּ֝מִשְׁפָּ֗ט לֹ֣א יִמְעַל־פִּֽיו׃
*Qesem al-siftei-melekh, be-mishpat lo yim'al-piv.*
There is magic on the lips of the king; he cannot err in judgment.

**11**
פֶּ֤לֶס וּמֹאזְנֵ֣י מִ֭שְׁפָּט לַיהֹוָ֑ה מַ֝עֲשֵׂ֗הוּ כׇּל־אַבְנֵי־כִֽיס׃
*Peles u-mo'znei mishpat la-Adonai, ma'asehu kol-avnei-kis.*
Honest scales and balances are GOD's, whose work includes all the weights in the bag.

**12**
תּוֹעֲבַ֣ת מְ֭לָכִים עֲשׂ֣וֹת רֶ֑שַׁע כִּ֥י בִ֝צְדָקָ֗ה יִכּ֥וֹן כִּסֵּֽא׃
*To'avat melakhim asot resha, ki vi-tzedaqah yikkon kisse.*
Wicked deeds are an abomination to kings, for the throne is established by righteousness.

**13**
רְצ֣וֹן מְ֭לָכִים שִׂפְתֵי־צֶ֑דֶק וְדֹבֵ֖ר יְשָׁרִ֣ים יֶאֱהָֽב׃
*Retzon melakhim siftei-tzedeq, ve-dover yesharim ye'ehav.*
Truthful speech wins the favor of kings; they love those who speak honestly.

**14**
חֲמַת־מֶ֥לֶךְ מַלְאֲכֵי־מָ֑וֶת וְאִ֖ישׁ חָכָ֣ם יְכַפְּרֶֽנָּה׃
*Chamat-melekh mal'akhei-mavet, ve-ish chakham yekhapperenah.*
The king's wrath is a messenger of death, but a man who is wise can appease it.

**15**
בְּאוֹר־פְּנֵי־מֶ֥לֶךְ חַיִּ֑ים וּ֝רְצוֹנ֗וֹ כְּעָ֣ב מַלְקֽוֹשׁ׃
*Be-or-penei-melekh chayyim, u-retzona ke-av malqosh.*
The king's smile means life; his favor is like a rain cloud in spring.

**16**
קְֽנֹה־חׇכְמָ֗ה מַה־טּ֥וֹב מֵחָר֑וּץ וּקְנ֥וֹת בִּ֝ינָ֗ה נִבְחָ֥ר מִכָּֽסֶף׃
*Qenoh-chokhmah mah-tov me-charutz, u-qenot vinah nivchar mi-kasef.*
How much better to acquire wisdom than gold; to acquire understanding is preferable to silver.

**17**
מְסִלַּ֣ת יְ֭שָׁרִים ס֣וּר מֵרָ֑ע שֹׁמֵ֥ר נַ֝פְשׁ֗וֹ נֹצֵ֥ר דַּרְכּֽוֹ׃
*Mesilat yesharim sur me-ra, shomer nafsho notzer darko.*
The highway of the upright avoids evil; those who would preserve their lives watch their way.

**18**
לִפְנֵי־שֶׁ֥בֶר גָּא֑וֹן וְלִפְנֵ֥י כִ֝שָּׁל֗וֹן גֹּ֣בַהּ רֽוּחַ׃
*Lifnei-shever ga'on, ve-lifnei kishalon govah ruach.*
Pride goes before ruin, arrogance before failure.

**19**
טֽוֹב שְׁפַל־ר֭וּחַ אֶת־עֲנָוִ֑ים מֵחַלֵּ֥ק שָׁ֝לָ֗ל אֶת־גֵּאִֽים׃
*Tov shefal-ruach et-anavim, me-challeq shalal et-ge'im.*
Better to be humble and among the lowly than to share spoils with the proud.

**20**
מַשְׂכִּ֣יל עַל־דָּ֭בָר יִמְצָא־ט֑וֹב וּבוֹטֵ֖חַ בַּיהֹוָ֣ה אַשְׁרָֽיו׃
*Maskil al-davar yimtza-tov, u-vote'ach ba-Adonai ashrav.*
One who is adept in a matter will attain success; happy is the one who trusts in GOD.

**21**
לַֽחֲכַם־לֵ֭ב יִקָּרֵ֣א נָב֑וֹן וּמֶ֥תֶק שְׂ֝פָתַ֗יִם יֹסִ֥יף לֶֽקַח׃
*La-chakham-lev yiqqare navon, u-meteq sefatayim yosif leqach.*
The wise-hearted are called discerning; those whose speech is pleasing gain wisdom.

**22**
מְק֣וֹר חַ֭יִּים שֵׂ֣כֶל בְּעָלָ֑יו וּמוּסַ֖ר אֱוִלִ֣ים אִוֶּֽלֶת׃
*Meqor chayyim sekhel be-alav, u-musar evilim ivvelet.*
Good sense is a fountain of life to those who have it, and folly is the punishment of fools.

**23**
לֵ֣ב חָ֭כָם יַשְׂכִּ֣יל פִּ֑יהוּ וְעַל־שְׂ֝פָתָ֗יו יֹסִ֥יף לֶֽקַח׃
*Lev chakham yaskil pihu, ve-al-sefatav yosif leqach.*
The mind of the wise makes their speech effective and increases the wisdom on their lips.

**24**
צוּף־דְּ֭בַשׁ אִמְרֵי־נֹ֑עַם מָת֥וֹק לַ֝נֶּ֗פֶשׁ וּמַרְפֵּ֥א לָעָֽצֶם׃
*Tzuf-devash imrei-no'am, matok la-nefesh u-marpe la-atzem.*
Pleasant words are like a honeycomb, sweet to the palate and a cure for the body.

**25**
יֵ֤שׁ דֶּ֣רֶךְ יָ֭שָׁר לִפְנֵי־אִ֑ישׁ וְ֝אַחֲרִיתָ֗הּ דַּרְכֵי־מָֽוֶת׃
*Yesh derekh yashar lifnei-ish, ve-acharitah darkhei-mavet.*
The road ahead may seem right, but in the end it can be a road to death.

**26**
נֶ֣פֶשׁ עָ֭מֵל עָ֣מְלָה לּ֑וֹ כִּֽי־אָכַ֖ף עָלָ֣יו פִּֽיהוּ׃
*Nefesh amel amlah lo, ki-akhaf alav pihu.*
The appetite of laborers labors for them, because their hunger forces them on.

**27**
אִ֣ישׁ בְּ֭לִיַּעַל כֹּרֶ֣ה רָעָ֑ה וְעַל־שְׂ֝פָת֗וֹ כְּאֵ֣שׁ צָרָֽבֶת׃
*Ish beliyya'al koreh ra'ah, ve-al-sefato ke-esh tzaravet.*
A worthless man plots evil; what is on his lips is like a scorching fire.

**28**
אִ֣ישׁ תַּ֭הְפֻּכוֹת יְשַׁלַּ֣ח מָד֑וֹן וְ֝נִרְגָּ֗ן מַפְרִ֥יד אַלּֽוּף׃
*Ish tahpukhot yeshallach madon, ve-nirgan mafrid alluf.*
A shifty man stirs up strife, and a querulous one alienates his friend.

**29**
אִ֣ישׁ חָ֭מָס יְפַתֶּ֣ה רֵעֵ֑הוּ וְ֝הוֹלִיכ֗וֹ בְּדֶ֣רֶךְ לֹא־טֽוֹב׃
*Ish chamas yefateh re'ehu, ve-holikho be-derekh lo-tov.*
A lawless man misleads his friend, making him take the wrong way.

**30**
עֹצֶ֣ה עֵ֭ינָיו לַחְשֹׁ֣ב תַּהְפֻּכ֑וֹת קֹרֵ֥ץ שְׂ֝פָתָ֗יו כִּלָּ֥ה רָעָֽה׃
*Otzeh einav lachshov tahpukhot, qorets sefatav killah ra'ah.*
He closes his eyes while meditating deception; he purses his lips while deciding upon evil.

**31**
עֲטֶ֣רֶת תִּפְאֶ֣רֶת שֵׂיבָ֑ה בְּדֶ֥רֶךְ צְ֝דָקָ֗ה תִּמָּצֵֽא׃
*Ateret tif'eret seivah, be-derekh tzedaqah timmatzei.*
Gray hair is a crown of glory; it is attained by the way of righteousness.

**32**
טֽוֹב אֶ֣רֶךְ אַ֭פַּיִם מִגִּבּ֑וֹר וּמֹשֵׁ֥ל בְּ֝רוּח֗וֹ מִלֹּכֵ֥ד עִֽיר׃
*Tov erekh appayim mi-gibbor, u-moshel be-rucho mi-lokhed ir.*
Better to be forbearing than mighty, to have self-control than to conquer a city.

**33**
בַּ֭חֵיק יוּטַ֣ל אֶת־הַגּוֹרָ֑ל וּ֝מֵיְהֹוָ֗ה כׇּל־מִשְׁפָּטֽוֹ׃
*Ba-cheiq yutal et-ha-goral, u-me-Adonai kol-mishpato.*
Lots are cast into the lap; the decision depends on GOD.
