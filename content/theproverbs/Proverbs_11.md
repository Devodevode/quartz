## Verses 1–31: Righteousness and Its Rewards

**1**
מֹאזְנֵ֣י מִ֭רְמָה תּוֹעֲבַ֣ת יְהֹוָ֑ה וְאֶ֖בֶן שְׁלֵמָ֣ה רְצוֹנֽוֹ׃
*Mo'znei mirmah to'avat Adonai, ve-even shelemah retzona.*
False scales are an abomination to GOD, who finds an honest weight pleasing.

**2**
בָּֽא־זָ֭דוֹן וַיָּבֹ֣א קָל֑וֹן וְֽאֶת־צְנוּעִ֥ים חׇכְמָֽה׃
*Ba-zadon va-yavo qalon, ve-et-tzenu'im chokhmah.*
When arrogance appears, disgrace follows, but wisdom is with those who are unassuming.

**3**
תֻּמַּ֣ת יְשָׁרִ֣ים תַּנְחֵ֑ם וְסֶ֖לֶף בֹּגְדִ֣ים יְשׇׁדֵּֽם׃
*Tummat yesharim tanchem, ve-selef bogdim yeshaddem.*
The integrity of the upright guides them; the deviousness of the treacherous leads them to ruin.

**4**
לֹא־יוֹעִ֣יל ה֭וֹן בְּי֣וֹם עֶבְרָ֑ה וּ֝צְדָקָ֗ה תַּצִּ֥יל מִמָּֽוֶת׃
*Lo-yo'il hon be-yom evrah, u-tzedaqah tatzil mi-mavet.*
Wealth is of no avail on the day of wrath, but righteousness saves from death.

**5**
צִדְקַ֣ת תָּ֭מִים תְּיַשֵּׁ֣ר דַּרְכּ֑וֹ וּ֝בְרִשְׁעָת֗וֹ יִפֹּ֥ל רָשָֽׁע׃
*Tzidqat tamim teyashsher darko, u-ve-rishato yippol rasha.*
The righteousness of the blameless smooths their way, but the wicked fall by their wickedness.

**6**
צִדְקַ֣ת יְ֭שָׁרִים תַּצִּילֵ֑ם וּ֝בְהַוַּ֗ת בֹּגְדִ֥ים יִלָּכֵֽדוּ׃
*Tzidqat yesharim tatzilem, u-ve-havvat bogdim yillakedu.*
The righteousness of the upright saves them, but the treacherous are trapped by their malice.

**7**
בְּמ֤וֹת אָדָ֣ם רָ֭שָׁע תֹּאבַ֣ד תִּקְוָ֑ה וְתוֹחֶ֖לֶת אוֹנִ֣ים אָבָֽדָה׃
*Be-mot adam rasha to'vad tiqvah, ve-tochellet onim avadah.*
At death the hopes of a wicked person are doomed, and the ambition of evildoers comes to nothing.

**8**
צַ֭דִּיק מִצָּרָ֣ה נֶחֱלָ֑ץ וַיָּבֹ֖א רָשָׁ֣ע תַּחְתָּֽיו׃
*Tzaddiq mi-tzarah nechelatz, va-yavo rasha tachtav.*
A righteous person is rescued from trouble and it comes to the wicked instead.

**9**
בְּפֶ֗ה חָ֭נֵף יַשְׁחִ֣ת רֵעֵ֑הוּ וּ֝בְדַ֗עַת צַדִּיקִ֥ים יֵחָלֵֽצוּ׃
*Be-feh chanef yashchit re'ehu, u-ve-da'at tzaddiqim yechalletzu.*
An impious person destroys a neighbor through speech, but through knowledge the righteous are rescued.

**10**
בְּט֣וּב צַ֭דִּיקִים תַּעֲלֹ֣ץ קִרְיָ֑ה וּבַאֲבֹ֖ד רְשָׁעִ֣ים רִנָּֽה׃
*Be-tuv tzaddiqim ta'aloz qiryah, u-va-avod resha'im rinnah.*
When the righteous prosper the city exults; when the wicked perish there are shouts of joy.

**11**
בְּבִרְכַּ֣ת יְ֭שָׁרִים תָּר֣וּם קָ֑רֶת וּבְפִ֥י רְ֝שָׁעִ֗ים תֵּהָרֵֽס׃
*Be-virkat yesharim tarum qaret, u-ve-fi resha'im tehareis.*
A city is built up by the blessing of the upright, but it is torn down by the speech of the wicked.

**12**
בׇּז־לְרֵעֵ֥הוּ חֲסַר־לֵ֑ב וְאִ֖ישׁ תְּבוּנ֣וֹת יַחֲרִֽישׁ׃
*Baz-le-re'ehu chasar-lev, ve-ish tevunot yacharish.*
One who speaks contemptuously of neighbors is devoid of sense; a prudent man keeps his peace.

**13**
הוֹלֵ֣ךְ רָ֭כִיל מְגַלֶּה־סּ֑וֹד וְנֶאֱמַן־ר֝֗וּחַ מְכַסֶּ֥ה דָבָֽר׃
*Holekh rakhil megalleh-sod, ve-ne'eman-ruach mekhasse davar.*
A base fellow gives away secrets, but a trustworthy soul keeps a confidence.

**14**
בְּאֵ֣ין תַּ֭חְבֻּלוֹת יִפׇּל־עָ֑ם וּ֝תְשׁוּעָ֗ה בְּרֹ֣ב יוֹעֵֽץ׃
*Be-ein tachbulot yippal-am, u-teshu'ah be-rov yo'etz.*
For want of strategy an army falls, but victory comes with much planning.

**15**
רַע־יֵ֭רוֹעַ כִּי־עָ֣רַב זָ֑ר וְשֹׂנֵ֖א תֹקְעִ֣ים בּוֹטֵֽחַ׃
*Ra-yero'a ki-arav zar, ve-sonei toqe'im boteach.*
Harm awaits one who stands surety for another; one who spurns pledging shall be secure.

**16**
אֵֽשֶׁת־חֵ֭ן תִּתְמֹ֣ךְ כָּב֑וֹד וְ֝עָרִיצִ֗ים יִתְמְכוּ־עֹֽשֶׁר׃
*Eshet-chen titmokh kavod, ve-aritzim yitmekhu-osher.*
A graceful woman obtains honor; ruthless men obtain wealth.

**17**
גֹּמֵ֣ל נַ֭פְשׁוֹ אִ֣ישׁ חָ֑סֶד וְעֹכֵ֥ר שְׁ֝אֵר֗וֹ אַכְזָרִֽי׃
*Gomel nafsho ish chesed, ve-okher she'ero akhzari.*
A kindly man benefits himself; a cruel one makes trouble for himself.

**18**
רָשָׁ֗ע עֹשֶׂ֥ה פְעֻלַּת־שָׁ֑קֶר וְזֹרֵ֥עַ צְ֝דָקָ֗ה שֶׂ֣כֶר אֱמֶֽת׃
*Rasha oseh fe'ullat-shaqer, ve-zore'a tzedaqah sekher emet.*
A wicked person earns illusory wages, but one who sows righteousness has a true reward.

**19**
כֵּן־צְדָקָ֥ה לְחַיִּ֑ים וּמְרַדֵּ֖ף רָעָ֣ה לְמוֹתֽוֹ׃
*Ken-tzedaqah le-chayyim, u-meraddef ra'ah le-moto.*
Righteousness is a prop of life, but to pursue evil leads to death.

**20**
תּוֹעֲבַ֣ת יְ֭הֹוָה עִקְּשֵׁי־לֵ֑ב וּ֝רְצוֹנ֗וֹ תְּמִ֣ימֵי דָֽרֶךְ׃
*To'avat Adonai iqqeshei-lev, u-retzona temimei darekh.*
Crooked minds are an abomination to GOD, but those whose way is blameless are pleasing.

**21**
יָ֣ד לְ֭יָד לֹא־יִנָּ֣קֶה רָּ֑ע וְזֶ֖רַע צַדִּיקִ֣ים נִמְלָֽט׃
*Yad le-yad lo-yinnaqeh ra, ve-zera tzaddiqim nimlat.*
Assuredly, the evildoer will not escape, but the offspring of the righteous will be safe.

**22**
נֶ֣זֶם זָ֭הָב בְּאַ֣ף חֲזִ֑יר אִשָּׁ֥ה יָ֝פָ֗ה וְסָ֣רַת טָֽעַם׃
*Nezem zahav be-af chazir, ishah yafah ve-sarat ta'am.*
Like a gold ring in the snout of a pig is a beautiful woman bereft of sense.

**23**
תַּאֲוַ֣ת צַדִּיקִ֣ים אַךְ־ט֑וֹב תִּקְוַ֖ת רְשָׁעִ֣ים עֶבְרָֽה׃
*Ta'avat tzaddiqim akh-tov, tiqvat resha'im evrah.*
What the righteous desire can only be good; what the wicked hope for stirs wrath.

**24**
יֵ֣שׁ מְ֭פַזֵּר וְנוֹסָ֥ף ע֑וֹד וְחֹשֵׂ֥ךְ מִ֝יֹּ֗שֶׁר אַךְ־לְמַחְסֽוֹר׃
*Yesh mefazzer ve-nosaf od, ve-chosekh mi-yyosher akh-le-machsor.*
Some give generously and end with more; others stint on doing the right thing and incur a loss.

**25**
נֶֽפֶשׁ־בְּרָכָ֥ה תְדֻשָּׁ֑ן וּ֝מַרְוֶ֗ה גַּם־ה֥וּא יוֹרֶֽא׃
*Nefesh-berakhah tedushan, u-marveh gam-hu yoreh.*
A generous person enjoys prosperity; one who satisfies others shall likewise be sated.

**26**
מֹ֣נֵֽעַ בָּ֭ר יִקְּבֻ֣הוּ לְא֑וֹם וּ֝בְרָכָ֗ה לְרֹ֣אשׁ מַשְׁבִּֽיר׃
*Mone'a bar yiqqevuhu le'om, u-verakhah le-rosh mashbir.*
Whoever withholds grain earns the curses of the people, but blessings are on the head of the one who dispenses it.

**27**
שֹׁ֣חֵֽר ט֭וֹב יְבַקֵּ֣שׁ רָצ֑וֹן וְדֹרֵ֖שׁ רָעָ֣ה תְבוֹאֶֽנּוּ׃
*Shocher tov yevaqesh ratzon, ve-doresh ra'ah tevo'ennu.*
One who earnestly seeks good pursues what is pleasing; evil comes to someone who is bent on it.

**28**
בּוֹטֵ֣חַ בְּ֭עׇשְׁרוֹ ה֣וּא יִפּ֑וֹל וְ֝כֶעָלֶ֗ה צַדִּיקִ֥ים יִפְרָֽחוּ׃
*Bote'ach be-oshro hu yippol, ve-khe-aleh tzaddiqim yifrachu.*
One who trusts in wealth shall fall, but the righteous shall flourish like foliage.

**29**
עֹכֵ֣ר בֵּ֭יתוֹ יִנְחַל־ר֑וּחַ וְעֶ֥בֶד אֱ֝וִ֗יל לַֽחֲכַם־לֵֽב׃
*Okher beito yinchal-ruach, ve-eved evil la-chakham-lev.*
He who makes trouble for his household shall inherit the wind; a fool is a slave to the wise-hearted.

**30**
פְּֽרִי־צַ֭דִּיק עֵ֣ץ חַיִּ֑ים וְלֹקֵ֖חַ נְפָשׁ֣וֹת חָכָֽם׃
*Peri-tzaddiq etz chayyim, ve-loqeach nefashot chakham.*
The fruit of the righteous is a tree of life; the wise captivate people.

**31**
הֵ֣ן צַ֭דִּיק בָּאָ֣רֶץ יְשֻׁלָּ֑ם אַ֝֗ף כִּֽי־רָשָׁ֥ע וְחוֹטֵֽא׃
*Hen tzaddiq ba-aretz yeshullam, af ki-rasha ve-chote.*
If the righteous on earth get their deserts, how much more evildoers and sinners.
