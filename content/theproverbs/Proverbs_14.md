## Verses 1–35: The Wise and the Foolish

**1**
חַכְמ֣וֹת נָ֭שִׁים בָּנְתָ֣ה בֵיתָ֑הּ וְ֝אִוֶּ֗לֶת בְּיָדֶ֥יהָ תֶהֶרְסֶֽנּוּ׃
*Chakhmot nashim bantah veittah, ve-ivvelet be-yadeha teharsennu.*
The wisest of women builds her house, but folly tears it down with its own hands.

**2**
הוֹלֵ֣ךְ בְּ֭יׇשְׁרוֹ יְרֵ֣א יְהֹוָ֑ה וּנְל֖וֹז דְּרָכָ֣יו בּוֹזֵֽהוּ׃
*Holekh be-yoshro yere Adonai, u-neloz derakhav bozeihu.*
One who walks a straight path fears GOD; but one whose ways are devious shows scorn.

**3**
בְּֽפִי־אֱ֭וִיל חֹ֣טֶר גַּאֲוָ֑ה וְשִׂפְתֵ֥י חֲ֝כָמִ֗ים תִּשְׁמוּרֵֽם׃
*Be-fi-evil choter ga'avah, ve-siftei chakhamim tishmurem.*
In the mouth of a fool is a rod of haughtiness, but the lips of the wise protect them.

**4**
בְּאֵ֣ין אֲ֭לָפִים אֵב֣וּס בָּ֑ר וְרׇב־תְּ֝בוּא֗וֹת בְּכֹ֣חַ שֽׁוֹר׃
*Be-ein alafim evus bar, ve-rav-tevuot be-koach shor.*
If there are no oxen the crib is clean, but a rich harvest comes through the strength of the ox.

**5**
עֵ֣ד אֱ֭מוּנִים לֹ֣א יְכַזֵּ֑ב וְיָפִ֥יחַ כְּ֝זָבִ֗ים עֵ֣ד שָֽׁקֶר׃
*Ed emunim lo yekhazzev, ve-yafiach kezavim ed shaqer.*
An honest witness will not lie; a false witness testifies lies.

**6**
בִּקֶּשׁ־לֵ֣ץ חׇכְמָ֣ה וָאָ֑יִן וְדַ֖עַת לְנָב֣וֹן נָקָֽל׃
*Biqqesh-letz chokhmah va-ayin, ve-da'at le-navon naqal.*
Scoffers seek wisdom in vain, but knowledge comes easily to the intelligent.

**7**
לֵ֣ךְ מִ֭נֶּגֶד לְאִ֣ישׁ כְּסִ֑יל וּבַל־יָ֝דַ֗עְתָּ שִׂפְתֵי־דָֽעַת׃
*Lekh mi-neged le-ish kesil, u-val-yadata siftei-da'at.*
Keep your distance from a dull man, for you will not learn wise speech.

**8**
חׇכְמַ֣ת עָ֭רוּם הָבִ֣ין דַּרְכּ֑וֹ וְאִוֶּ֖לֶת כְּסִילִ֣ים מִרְמָֽה׃
*Chokmat arum havin darko, ve-ivvelet kesilim mirmah.*
It is the wisdom of a clever man to understand his course; but the stupidity of dullards is delusion.

**9**
אֱ֭וִלִים יָלִ֣יץ אָשָׁ֑ם וּבֵ֖ין יְשָׁרִ֣ים רָצֽוֹן׃
*Evilim yalitz asham, u-vein yesharim ratzon.*
Reparations mediate between fools; between the upright, good will.

**10**
לֵ֗ב י֭וֹדֵעַ מׇרַּ֣ת נַפְשׁ֑וֹ וּ֝בְשִׂמְחָת֗וֹ לֹא־יִתְעָ֥רַב זָֽר׃
*Lev yodea marrat nafsho, u-ve-simchato lo-yit'arav zar.*
The heart alone knows its bitterness, and no outsider can share in its joy.

**11**
בֵּ֣ית רְ֭שָׁעִים יִשָּׁמֵ֑ד וְאֹ֖הֶל יְשָׁרִ֣ים יַפְרִֽיחַ׃
*Beit resha'im yishammed, ve-ohel yesharim yafriach.*
The house of the wicked will be demolished, but the tent of the upright will flourish.

**12**
יֵ֤שׁ דֶּ֣רֶךְ יָ֭שָׁר לִפְנֵי־אִ֑ישׁ וְ֝אַחֲרִיתָ֗הּ דַּרְכֵי־מָֽוֶת׃
*Yesh derekh yashar lifnei-ish, ve-acharitah darkhei-mavet.*
The road ahead may seem right, but in the end it can be a road to death.

**13**
גַּם־בִּשְׂחֹ֥ק יִכְאַב־לֵ֑ב וְאַחֲרִיתָ֖הּ שִׂמְחָ֣ה תוּגָֽה׃
*Gam-be-sechoq yikh'av-lev, ve-acharitah simchah tugah.*
The heart may ache even in laughter, and joy may end in grief.

**14**
מִדְּרָכָ֣יו יִ֭שְׂבַּע ס֣וּג לֵ֑ב וּ֝מֵעָלָ֗יו אִ֣ישׁ טֽוֹב׃
*Mi-derakhav yisba sug lev, u-me'alav ish tov.*
Those who are unprincipled reap the fruits of their ways; the good man, of his deeds.

**15**
פֶּ֭תִי יַאֲמִ֣ין לְכׇל־דָּבָ֑ר וְ֝עָר֗וּם יָבִ֥ין לַאֲשֻׁרֽוֹ׃
*Peti ya'amin le-khol-davar, ve-arum yavin la-ashuро.*
The simple believe anything; the clever ponders their course.

**16**
חָכָ֣ם יָ֭רֵא וְסָ֣ר מֵרָ֑ע וּ֝כְסִ֗יל מִתְעַבֵּ֥ר וּבוֹטֵֽחַ׃
*Chakham yare ve-sar me-ra, u-khesil mit'abber u-vote'ach.*
A sage is diffident and shuns evil, but a dullard rushes in confidently.

**17**
קְֽצַר־אַ֭פַּיִם יַעֲשֶׂ֣ה אִוֶּ֑לֶת וְאִ֥ישׁ מְ֝זִמּ֗וֹת יִשָּׂנֵֽא׃
*Qetzar-appayim ya'aseh ivvelet, ve-ish mezimmot yissane.*
A hothead commits folly; a man of intrigues will be hated.

**18**
נָחֲל֣וּ פְתָאיִ֣ם אִוֶּ֑לֶת וַ֝עֲרוּמִ֗ים יַכְתִּ֥רוּ דָֽעַת׃
*Nachalu feta'yim ivvelet, va-arumim yakhтiru da'at.*
Folly is the lot of the simple, but the clever glory in knowledge.

**19**
שַׁח֣וּ רָ֭עִים לִפְנֵ֣י טוֹבִ֑ים וּ֝רְשָׁעִ֗ים עַֽל־שַׁעֲרֵ֥י צַדִּֽיק׃
*Shachu ra'im lifnei tovim, u-resha'im al-sha'arei tzaddiq.*
Evildoers are brought low before the good, so are the wicked at the gates of the righteous.

**20**
גַּם־לְ֭רֵעֵהוּ יִשָּׂ֣נֵא רָ֑שׁ וְאֹהֲבֵ֖י עָשִׁ֣יר רַבִּֽים׃
*Gam-le-re'ehu yissane rash, ve-ohavei ashir rabbim.*
The poor are despised even by their peers, but the rich have many friends.

**21**
בׇּז־לְרֵעֵ֥הוּ חוֹטֵ֑א וּמְחוֹנֵ֖ן עֲנָוִ֣ים אַשְׁרָֽיו׃
*Baz-le-re'ehu chote, u-mechonen anavim ashrav.*
Those who despise their fellows are wrong; those who show pity for the lowly are happy.

**22**
הֲֽלוֹא־יִ֭תְעוּ חֹ֣רְשֵׁי רָ֑ע וְחֶ֥סֶד וֶ֝אֱמֶ֗ת חֹ֣רְשֵׁי טֽוֹב׃
*Halo-yit'u chorshei ra, ve-chesed ve-emet chorshei tov.*
Surely those who plan evil go astray, while those who plan good earn steadfast love.

**23**
בְּכׇל־עֶ֭צֶב יִהְיֶ֣ה מוֹתָ֑ר וּדְבַר־שְׂ֝פָתַ֗יִם אַךְ־לְמַחְסֽוֹר׃
*Be-khol-etzev yihyeh motar, u-devar-sefatayim akh-le-machsor.*
From all toil there is some gain, but idle chatter is pure loss.

**24**
עֲטֶ֣רֶת חֲכָמִ֣ים עׇשְׁרָ֑ם אִוֶּ֖לֶת כְּסִילִ֣ים אִוֶּֽלֶת׃
*Ateret chakhamim oshram, ivvelet kesilim ivvelet.*
The ornament of the wise is their wealth; the stupidity of dullards is stupidity.

**25**
מַצִּ֣יל נְ֭פָשׁוֹת עֵ֣ד אֱמֶ֑ת וְיָפִ֖חַ כְּזָבִ֣ים מִרְמָֽה׃
*Matzil nefashot ed emet, ve-yafiach kezavim mirmah.*
A truthful witness saves lives; a lying witness spreads deceit.

**26**
בְּיִרְאַ֣ת יְ֭הֹוָה מִבְטַח־עֹ֑ז וּ֝לְבָנָ֗יו יִהְיֶ֥ה מַחְסֶֽה׃
*Be-yir'at Adonai mivtach-oz, u-le-vanav yihyeh machseh.*
Fear of GOD is a stronghold, a refuge for one's children.

**27**
יִרְאַ֣ת יְ֭הֹוָה מְק֣וֹר חַיִּ֑ים לָ֝ס֗וּר מִמֹּ֥קְשֵׁי מָֽוֶת׃
*Yir'at Adonai meqor chayyim, lasur mi-moqshei mavet.*
Fear of GOD is a fountain of life, enabling one to avoid deadly snares.

**28**
בְּרׇב־עָ֥ם הַדְרַת־מֶ֑לֶךְ וּבְאֶ֥פֶס לְ֝אֹ֗ם מְחִתַּ֥ת רָזֽוֹן׃
*Be-rav-am hadrat-melekh, u-ve-efes le'om mechittat razon.*
A numerous people is the glory of a king; without a nation a ruler is ruined.

**29**
אֶ֣רֶךְ אַ֭פַּיִם רַב־תְּבוּנָ֑ה וּקְצַר־ר֝֗וּחַ מֵרִ֥ים אִוֶּֽלֶת׃
*Erekh appayim rav-tevunah, u-qetzar-ruach merim ivvelet.*
Patience results in much understanding; impatience gets folly as its portion.

**30**
חַיֵּ֣י בְ֭שָׂרִים לֵ֣ב מַרְפֵּ֑א וּרְקַ֖ב עֲצָמ֣וֹת קִנְאָֽה׃
*Chayyei vesarim lev marpe, u-reqav atzamot qin'ah.*
A calm disposition gives bodily health; passion is rot to the bones.

**31**
עֹ֣שֵֽׁק דָּ֭ל חֵרֵ֣ף עֹשֵׂ֑הוּ וּ֝מְכַבְּד֗וֹ חֹנֵ֥ן אֶבְיֽוֹן׃
*Osheq dal cheref osehu, u-mekhabbedo chonen evyon.*
One who withholds what is due to the poor affronts their Maker; one who shows pity for the needy shows honor.

**32**
בְּֽ֭רָעָתוֹ יִדָּחֶ֣ה רָשָׁ֑ע וְחֹסֶ֖ה בְמוֹת֣וֹ צַדִּֽיק׃
*Be-ra'ato yiddacheh rasha, ve-choseh be-moto tzaddiq.*
The wicked fall by their own evil; the righteous find security in their death.

**33**
בְּלֵ֣ב נָ֭בוֹן תָּנ֣וּחַ חׇכְמָ֑ה וּבְקֶ֥רֶב כְּ֝סִילִ֗ים תִּוָּדֵֽעַ׃
*Be-lev navon tanuach chokhmah, u-ve-qerev kesilim tivvadea.*
Wisdom rests quietly in the mind of a prudent person, but among dullards it makes itself known.

**34**
צְדָקָ֥ה תְרוֹמֵֽם־גּ֑וֹי וְחֶ֖סֶד לְאֻמִּ֣ים חַטָּֽאת׃
*Tzedaqah teromem-goy, ve-chesed le'ummim chattot.*
Righteousness exalts a nation; sin is a reproach to any people.

**35**
רְֽצוֹן־מֶ֭לֶךְ לְעֶ֣בֶד מַשְׂכִּ֑יל וְ֝עֶבְרָת֗וֹ תִּהְיֶ֥ה מֵבִֽישׁ׃
*Retzon-melekh le-eved maskil, ve-evrato tihyeh meivish.*
The king favors a capable servant; he rages at an incompetent one.
