## Verses 1–6: Wisdom's Feast

**1**
חׇ֭כְמוֹת בָּנְתָ֣ה בֵיתָ֑הּ חָצְבָ֖ה עַמּוּדֶ֣יהָ שִׁבְעָֽה׃
*Chokhmot bantah veittah, chatzevah ammudeiha shiv'ah.*
Wisdom has built her house, she has hewn her seven pillars.

**2**
טָבְחָ֣ה טִ֭בְחָהּ מָסְכָ֣ה יֵינָ֑הּ אַ֝֗ף עָרְכָ֥ה שֻׁלְחָנָֽהּ׃
*Tavchah tivchah maskah yeinah, af arkhah shulchanah.*
She has prepared the feast, mixed the wine, and also set the table.

**3**
שָׁלְחָ֣ה נַעֲרֹתֶ֣יהָ תִקְרָ֑א עַל־גַּ֝פֵּ֗י מְרֹ֣מֵי קָֽרֶת׃
*Shalchah na'aroteha tiqra, al-gappei meromei qaret.*
She has sent out her maids to announce on the heights of the town.

**4**
מִֽי־פֶ֭תִי יָסֻ֣ר הֵ֑נָּה חֲסַר־לֵ֝֗ב אָ֣מְרָה לּֽוֹ׃
*Mi-feti yasur hennah, chasar-lev amrah lo.*
"Let the simple enter here." To those devoid of sense she says:

**5**
לְ֭כוּ לַחֲמ֣וּ בְֽלַחֲמִ֑י וּ֝שְׁת֗וּ בְּיַ֣יִן מָסָֽכְתִּי׃
*Lekhu lachamu ve-lachami, u-shetu be-yayin masakti.*
"Come, eat my food and drink the wine that I have mixed.

**6**
עִזְב֣וּ פְתָאיִ֣ם וִֽחְי֑וּ וְ֝אִשְׁר֗וּ בְּדֶ֣רֶךְ בִּינָֽה׃
*Izvu feta'yim vi-cheyu, ve-ishru be-derekh vinah.*
Give up simpleness and live, walk in the way of understanding."

## Verses 7–12: The Wise and the Scoffer

**7**
יֹ֤סֵ֨ר ׀ לֵ֗ץ לֹקֵ֣חַֽ ל֣וֹ קָל֑וֹן וּמוֹכִ֖יחַ לְרָשָׁ֣ע מוּמֽוֹ׃
*Yoser letz loqeach lo qalon, u-mokhiach le-rasha mumo.*
To correct a scoffer, or rebuke an evildoer regarding a blemish, is to call down abuse on oneself.

**8**
אַל־תּ֣וֹכַח לֵ֭ץ פֶּן־יִשְׂנָאֶ֑ךָּ הוֹכַ֥ח לְ֝חָכָ֗ם וְיֶאֱהָבֶֽךָּ׃
*Al-tokhach letz pen-yisna'ekha, hokheach le-chakham ve-ye'ehavekha.*
Do not rebuke scoffers, for they will hate you; reprove the wise, and they will love you.

**9**
תֵּ֣ן לְ֭חָכָם וְיֶחְכַּם־ע֑וֹד הוֹדַ֥ע לְ֝צַדִּ֗יק וְי֣וֹסֶף לֶֽקַח׃
*Ten le-chakham ve-yechkam-od, hoda le-tzaddiq ve-yosef leqach.*
Instruct the wise, and they will grow wiser; teach the righteous, and they will gain in learning.

**10**
תְּחִלַּ֣ת חׇ֭כְמָה יִרְאַ֣ת יְהֹוָ֑ה וְדַ֖עַת קְדֹשִׁ֣ים בִּינָֽה׃
*Techillat chokhmah yir'at Adonai, ve-da'at qedoshim vinah.*
The beginning of wisdom is fear of GOD, and knowledge of the Holy One is understanding.

**11**
כִּי־בִ֭י יִרְבּ֣וּ יָמֶ֑יךָ וְיוֹסִ֥יפוּ לְּ֝ךָ֗ שְׁנ֣וֹת חַיִּֽים׃
*Ki-vi yirbu yamekha, ve-yosifu lekha shenot chayyim.*
For through me your days will increase, and years be added to your life.

**12**
אִם־חָ֭כַמְתָּ חָכַ֣מְתָּ לָּ֑ךְ וְ֝לַ֗צְתָּ לְֽבַדְּךָ֥ תִשָּֽׂא׃
*Im-chakhamta chakhamta lakh, ve-latzta le-vadekha tissa.*
If you are wise, you are wise for yourself; if you are a scoffer, you bear it alone.

## Verses 13–18: Folly's Rival Call

**13**
אֵ֣שֶׁת כְּ֭סִילוּת הֹמִיָּ֑ה פְּ֝תַיּ֗וּת וּבַל־יָ֥דְעָה מָּֽה׃
*Eshet kesilut homiyyah, peta'yut u-val-yadah mah.*
The stupid woman bustles about; she is simple and knows nothing.

**14**
וְֽ֭יָשְׁבָה לְפֶ֣תַח בֵּיתָ֑הּ עַל־כִּ֝סֵּ֗א מְרֹ֣מֵי קָֽרֶת׃
*Ve-yashevah le-fetach beitah, al-kisse meromei qaret.*
She sits in the doorway of her house, or on a chair at the heights of the town.

**15**
לִקְרֹ֥א לְעֹֽבְרֵי־דָ֑רֶךְ הַֽ֝מְיַשְּׁרִ֗ים אֹֽרְחוֹתָֽם׃
*Liqro le-ovrei-darekh, ha-meyashrim orchotam.*
Calling to all the wayfarers who go about their own affairs.

**16**
מִֽי־פֶ֭תִי יָסֻ֣ר הֵ֑נָּה וַחֲסַר־לֵ֝֗ב וְאָ֣מְרָה לּֽוֹ׃
*Mi-feti yasur hennah, va-chasar-lev ve-amrah lo.*
"Let the simple enter here"; and to those devoid of sense she says:

**17**
מַֽיִם־גְּנוּבִ֥ים יִמְתָּ֑קוּ וְלֶ֖חֶם סְתָרִ֣ים יִנְעָֽם׃
*Mayim-genuvim yimtaqu, ve-lechem setarim yin'am.*
"Stolen waters are sweet, and bread eaten furtively is tasty."

**18**
וְֽלֹא־יָ֭דַע כִּֽי־רְפָאִ֣ים שָׁ֑ם בְּעִמְקֵ֖י שְׁא֣וֹל קְרֻאֶֽיהָ׃
*Ve-lo-yada ki-refa'im sham, be-imqei she'ol qeru'eha.*
He does not know that the shades are there, that her guests are in the depths of Sheol.
