## Verses 1–32: The Proverbs of Solomon

**1**
מִשְׁלֵ֗י שְׁלֹ֫מֹ֥ה בֵּ֣ן חָ֭כָם יְשַׂמַּח־אָ֑ב וּבֵ֥ן כְּ֝סִ֗יל תּוּגַ֥ת אִמּֽוֹ׃
*Mishlei Shlomoh: ben chakham yesammach-av, u-ven kesil tugat immo.*
The proverbs of Solomon: A wise son brings joy to his father; a dull son is his mother's sorrow.

**2**
לֹֽא־י֭וֹעִילוּ אוֹצְר֣וֹת רֶ֑שַׁע וּ֝צְדָקָ֗ה תַּצִּ֥יל מִמָּֽוֶת׃
*Lo-yo'ilu otzrot resha, u-tzedaqah tatzil mi-mavet.*
Ill-gotten wealth is of no avail, but righteousness saves from death.

**3**
לֹא־יַרְעִ֣יב יְ֭הֹוָה נֶ֣פֶשׁ צַדִּ֑יק וְהַוַּ֖ת רְשָׁעִ֣ים יֶהְדֹּֽף׃
*Lo-yar'iv Adonai nefesh tzaddiq, ve-havvat resha'im yehdof.*
GOD will not let a righteous person go hungry, but denies the wicked what they crave.

**4**
רָ֗אשׁ עֹשֶׂ֥ה כַף־רְמִיָּ֑ה וְיַ֖ד חָרוּצִ֣ים תַּעֲשִֽׁיר׃
*Rash oseh khaf-remiyyah, ve-yad charuzim ta'ashir.*
Negligent hands cause poverty, but diligent hands enrich.

**5**
אֹגֵ֣ר בַּ֭קַּיִץ בֵּ֣ן מַשְׂכִּ֑יל נִרְדָּ֥ם בַּ֝קָּצִ֗יר בֵּ֣ן מֵבִֽישׁ׃
*Oger ba-qayitz ben maskil, nirdam ba-qatzir ben meivish.*
He who lays in stores during the summer is a capable son, but he who sleeps during the harvest is an incompetent.

**6**
בְּ֭רָכוֹת לְרֹ֣אשׁ צַדִּ֑יק וּפִ֥י רְ֝שָׁעִ֗ים יְכַסֶּ֥ה חָמָֽס׃
*Berakhot le-rosh tzaddiq, u-fi resha'im yekhasse chamas.*
Blessings light upon the head of a righteous person, but lawlessness covers the mouths of the wicked.

**7**
זֵ֣כֶר צַ֭דִּיק לִבְרָכָ֑ה וְשֵׁ֖ם רְשָׁעִ֣ים יִרְקָֽב׃
*Zekher tzaddiq li-vrakhah, ve-shem resha'im yirqav.*
The name of a righteous person is invoked in blessing, but the fame of the wicked rots.

**8**
חֲכַם־לֵ֭ב יִקַּ֣ח מִצְוֺ֑ת וֶאֱוִ֥יל שְׂ֝פָתַ֗יִם יִלָּבֵֽט׃
*Chakham-lev yiqqach mitzvot, ve-evil sefatayim yillavet.*
Whoever has a wise heart accepts commands, but one whose speech is foolish comes to grief.

**9**
הוֹלֵ֣ךְ בַּ֭תֹּם יֵ֣לֶךְ בֶּ֑טַח וּמְעַקֵּ֥שׁ דְּ֝רָכָ֗יו יִוָּדֵֽעַ׃
*Holekh ba-tom yelekh betach, u-me'aqesh derakhav yivvadea.*
Whoever lives blamelessly lives safely, but one who walks a crooked path will be found out.

**10**
קֹ֣רֵֽץ עַ֭יִן יִתֵּ֣ן עַצָּ֑בֶת וֶאֱוִ֥יל שְׂ֝פָתַ֗יִם יִלָּבֵֽט׃
*Qorets ayin yitten atztzevet, ve-evil sefatayim yillavet.*
Whoever winks causes sorrow; one whose speech is foolish comes to grief.

**11**
מְק֣וֹר חַ֭יִּים פִּ֣י צַדִּ֑יק וּפִ֥י רְ֝שָׁעִ֗ים יְכַסֶּ֥ה חָמָֽס׃
*Meqor chayyim pi tzaddiq, u-fi resha'im yekhasse chamas.*
The mouth of a righteous person is a fountain of life, but lawlessness covers the mouths of the wicked.

**12**
שִׂ֭נְאָה תְּעֹרֵ֣ר מְדָנִ֑ים וְעַ֥ל כׇּל־פְּ֝שָׁעִ֗ים תְּכַסֶּ֥ה אַהֲבָֽה׃
*Sin'ah te'orer medanim, ve-al kol-pesha'im tekhasse ahavah.*
Hatred stirs up strife, but love covers up all faults.

**13**
בְּשִׂפְתֵ֣י נָ֭בוֹן תִּמָּצֵ֣א חׇכְמָ֑ה וְ֝שֵׁ֗בֶט לְגֵ֣ו חֲסַר־לֵֽב׃
*Be-siftei navon timmatzei chokhmah, ve-shevet le-gev chasar-lev.*
Wisdom is to be found on the lips of the intelligent, but a rod is ready for the back of the senseless.

**14**
חֲכָמִ֥ים יִצְפְּנוּ־דָ֑עַת וּפִֽי־אֱ֝וִ֗יל מְחִתָּ֥ה קְרֹבָֽה׃
*Chakhamim yitzpenu-da'at, u-fi-evil mechittah qerovah.*
The wise store up knowledge; the mouth of a fool is an imminent ruin.

**15**
ה֣וֹן עָ֭שִׁיר קִרְיַ֣ת עֻזּ֑וֹ מְחִתַּ֖ת דַּלִּ֣ים רֵישָֽׁם׃
*Hon ashir qiryat uzzo, mechittat dallim reisham.*
For someone rich, wealth is a fortress; for the poor, poverty is a ruin.

**16**
פְּעֻלַּ֣ת צַדִּ֣יק לְחַיִּ֑ים תְּבוּאַ֖ת רָשָׁ֣ע לְחַטָּֽאת׃
*Pe'ullat tzaddiq le-chayyim, tevuat rasha le-chattot.*
The labor of the righteous makes for life; the produce of the wicked makes for want.

**17**
אֹ֣רַח לְ֭חַיִּים שׁוֹמֵ֣ר מוּסָ֑ר וְעֹזֵ֖ב תּוֹכַ֣חַת מַתְעֶֽה׃
*Orach le-chayyim shomer musar, ve-ozev tokhechat mat'eh.*
One who follows discipline shows the way to life, but one who ignores reproof leads astray.

**18**
מְכַסֶּ֣ה שִׂ֭נְאָה שִׂפְתֵי־שָׁ֑קֶר וּמוֹצִ֥א דִ֝בָּ֗ה ה֣וּא כְסִֽיל׃
*Mekhasse sin'ah siftei-shaqer, u-motzi dibbah hu kesil.*
One who conceals hatred has lying lips, while one who speaks forth slander is a dullard.

**19**
בְּרֹ֣ב דְּ֭בָרִים לֹ֣א יֶחְדַּל־פָּ֑שַׁע וְחוֹשֵׂ֖ךְ שְׂפָתָ֣יו מַשְׂכִּֽיל׃
*Be-rov devarim lo yechdal-pasha, ve-chosekh sefatav maskil.*
Where there is much talking, there is no lack of transgressing, but one who curbs the tongue shows sense.

**20**
כֶּ֣סֶף נִ֭בְחָר לְשׁ֣וֹן צַדִּ֑יק לֵ֖ב רְשָׁעִ֣ים כִּמְעָֽט׃
*Kesef nivchar leshon tzaddiq, lev resha'im kim'at.*
The tongue of a righteous person is choice silver, but the mind of the wicked is of little worth.

**21**
שִׂפְתֵ֣י צַ֭דִּיק יִרְע֣וּ רַבִּ֑ים וֶ֝אֱוִילִ֗ים בַּחֲסַר־לֵ֥ב יָמֽוּתוּ׃
*Siftei tzaddiq yir'u rabbim, ve-evilim ba-chasar-lev yamutu.*
The lips of a righteous person sustain many, but fools die for lack of sense.

**22**
בִּרְכַּ֣ת יְ֭הֹוָה הִ֣יא תַעֲשִׁ֑יר וְלֹֽא־יוֹסִ֖ף עֶ֣צֶב עִמָּֽהּ׃
*Birkat Adonai hi ta'ashir, ve-lo-yosif etzev immah.*
It is the blessing of GOD that enriches, and no toil can increase it.

**23**
כִּשְׂח֣וֹק לִ֭כְסִיל עֲשׂ֣וֹת זִמָּ֑ה וְ֝חׇכְמָ֗ה לְאִ֣ישׁ תְּבוּנָֽה׃
*Ki-sechoq li-khesil asot zimmah, ve-chokhmah le-ish tevunah.*
As mischief is sport for the dullard, so is wisdom for the man of understanding.

**24**
מְגוֹרַ֣ת רָ֭שָׁע הִ֣יא תְבוֹאֶ֑נּוּ וְתַאֲוַ֖ת צַדִּיקִ֣ים יִתֵּֽן׃
*Megorat rasha hi tevo'ennu, ve-ta'avat tzaddiqim yitten.*
What an evildoer plots backfires; what the righteous desire is granted.

**25**
כַּעֲב֣וֹר ס֭וּפָה וְאֵ֣ין רָשָׁ֑ע וְ֝צַדִּ֗יק יְס֣וֹד עוֹלָֽם׃
*Ka-avor sufah ve-ein rasha, ve-tzaddiq yesod olam.*
When the storm passes a wicked person is gone, but the righteous person is an everlasting foundation.

**26**
כַּחֹ֤מֶץ ׀ לַשִּׁנַּ֗יִם וְכֶעָשָׁ֥ן לָעֵינָ֑יִם כֵּ֥ן הֶ֝עָצֵ֗ל לְשֹׁלְחָֽיו׃
*Ka-chometz la-shinnayim ve-khe-ashan la-einayim, ken he-atzel le-sholechav.*
Like vinegar to the teeth, like smoke to the eyes, are sluggards to those who send them on a mission.

**27**
יִרְאַ֣ת יְ֭הֹוָה תּוֹסִ֣יף יָמִ֑ים וּשְׁנ֖וֹת רְשָׁעִ֣ים תִּקְצֹֽרְנָה׃
*Yir'at Adonai tosif yamim, u-shenot resha'im tiqtzorenah.*
The fear of GOD prolongs life, while the years of the wicked will be shortened.

**28**
תּוֹחֶ֣לֶת צַדִּיקִ֣ים שִׂמְחָ֑ה וְתִקְוַ֖ת רְשָׁעִ֣ים תֹּאבֵֽד׃
*Tochellet tzaddiqim simchah, ve-tiqvat resha'im to'ved.*
The righteous can look forward to joy, but the hopes of the wicked are doomed.

**29**
מָע֣וֹז לַ֭תֹּם דֶּ֣רֶךְ יְהֹוָ֑ה וּ֝מְחִתָּ֗ה לְפֹ֣עֲלֵי אָֽוֶן׃
*Ma'oz la-tom derekh Adonai, u-mechittah le-fo'alei aven.*
The way of GOD is a stronghold for a blameless person, but a ruin for evildoers.

**30**
צַ֭דִּיק לְעוֹלָ֣ם בַּל־יִמּ֑וֹט וּ֝רְשָׁעִ֗ים לֹ֣א יִשְׁכְּנוּ־אָֽרֶץ׃
*Tzaddiq le-olam bal-yimmot, u-resha'im lo yishkenu-aretz.*
The righteous person will never be shaken; the wicked will not inhabit the earth.

**31**
פִּֽי־צַ֭דִּיק יָנ֣וּב חׇכְמָ֑ה וּלְשׁ֥וֹן תַּ֝הְפֻּכ֗וֹת תִּכָּרֵֽת׃
*Pi-tzaddiq yanub chokhmah, u-leshon tahpukhot tikkaret.*
The mouth of a righteous person produces wisdom, but the treacherous tongue shall be cut off.

**32**
שִׂפְתֵ֣י צַ֭דִּיק יֵדְע֣וּן רָצ֑וֹן וּפִ֥י רְ֝שָׁעִ֗ים תַּהְפֻּכֽוֹת׃
*Siftei tzaddiq yede'un ratzon, u-fi resha'im tahpukhot.*
The lips of a righteous person know what is pleasing; the mouth of the wicked knows duplicity.
