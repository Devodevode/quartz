Stage Two is Systematic Iteration: building and refining the method. Create V1, test it, analyze results, identify improvements, build V2. Ask constantly: how could this find MORE? Focus on single vulnerability types until reaching V10. Success is measured by iteration count and first consistent discoveries. Stage Three is Meta-Optimization: optimizing the optimization process itself. Use AI to identify unknown unknowns. Automate the discovery process. Apply successful patterns to new domains. Success is measured by exponential scaling and passive income generation. Most never reach Stage Two. Those who do often never reach Stage Three. Each stage requires different mindset. Stage One needs courage. Stage Two needs persistence. Stage Three needs meta-awareness. The stages are not optional; they are the path. Skipping Stage One because you want to think more dooms you. Stopping at Stage Two when bored prevents exponential. Only those who progress through all three reach V20.

## The Prerequisites Trap
The belief that you need X before you can do Y. That you must learn everything before attempting anything. That preparation precedes action. This trap kills more potential than any external obstacle. The truth: you do not need to understand the entire system before engaging with it. You do not need comprehensive knowledge before making first attempt. You do not need perfect clarity before starting. The prerequisites trap manifests as "I need to take this course first" or "I need to read these books first" or "I need to understand this concept first." All lies. The real prerequisite for learning is doing. Understanding emerges from action, not before it. Every hour spent preparing is an hour not spent learning. Every course watched is time not spent testing. The prerequisites trap is comfortable; it feels productive to learn without the risk of failure. But this comfort is the trap. You are learning about the thing instead of learning the thing. Start with zero preparation. Pick target. Test. Fail. Learn from failure. Iterate. This path is faster than any amount of preliminary study. The person who starts immediately and fails for seven days learns more than the person who studies for seven days before starting. Break the trap. Do it now. Prerequisites are procrastination disguised as responsibility.

## Success Indicators
Know what predicts success versus what feels like success. False indicators: courses completed, books read, time spent studying, knowledge accumulated, tools installed, methodologies memorized. These correlate weakly with results. True indicators: attempts made, failures documented, iterations completed, V-number reached, first bounty earned, consistent bug discovery, automated systems built, meta-optimization begun. If your success metric is how much you learned, you are measuring the wrong thing. If your success metric is what you earned or what you built, you are measuring correctly. Learning without earning is a trap. Knowledge without application is waste. The person with less knowledge but more iterations will outperform the expert who never ships. Judge yourself by outputs, not inputs. By results, not effort. By what persists, not what you experienced. Success indicators should be concrete, measurable, undeniable. Not "I understand this better" but "I found three bugs this week." Not "I'm getting comfortable with the tools" but "I automated the reconnaissance phase." Not "I'm learning a lot" but "I made two hundred dollars." The true indicators are uncomfortable because they cannot be faked. You either have results or you do not. Use this clarity as guidance; if the indicator is vague, it is likely false.
Productive incompleteness is the engine of all value. Voids are not problems to solve but fuel to harness. The gap between current and possible, between known and unknown, between V3 and V20: these gaps generate meaning, motivation, growth. If you arrived, if you achieved transcendence, if you made all unknowns known, you would stop. Meaning would collapse. The pursuit would end. The very incompleteness you fear is the source of everything worth having. Questions without final answers keep you questioning. Goals beyond reach keep you reaching. Ideals impossible to achieve keep you iterating. The void is the engine. The lack is the abundance. The productive struggle against impossible completion is life itself. Embrace the incompleteness. Chase what cannot be caught. Iterate toward what cannot be reached. Stop at V3 and you stop growing. Commit to V20 knowing you will never arrive; but the journey toward impossible creates everything possible.
